                                ***** PENGO RO *****
 

===INTRODUCTION========== 
 
Pengo Ro is a shareware recreation of one of my favorite arcade games of the 80's.  The 
grid size is somewhat smaller than on the original but the gameplay is much the same (at 
least as far as I can remember).

===INSTALLATION==========
 
Make a directory called C:/Documents/PengoRo and copy the following files to it:

     PengoRo.opo
     Bitmap.mbm
     Title.mbm


===PLAYING PENGO RO=========

Start the file "Pengo.opo" to run Pengo Ro.  You will see the title screen and then the 
brief instructions on how to play.  Press a key and you will then be allowed to enter your 
choice of keys to press for up/down/left/right.  If you make a mistake, press "n" when you 
are asked if you are happy with your key selections.  You will be given the opportunity to 
enter the key selections again.  The up arrow on the keyboard causes Pengo Ro to act 
strangely, so I would suggest using a different key for moving up.

After you select the level you would like to start on, the maze will be shown until you 
press a key.  At that time, the level begins.  (HINT: There are over 32000 levels.  You can 
access some levels > 9 by pressing keys other than numbers on the Psion Series 5 keyboard.)

If you die on a level, you will have to replay that level over again. 

You get an extra life on every level divisible by 3.
 
When you smash SnowBees with ice blocks, you get the following points based on the numbers 
of SnowBees smashed with a single block:
 
     1st SnowBee  100 pts.
     2nd SnowBee  200 pts.
     3rd SnowBee  400 pts.
     4th SnowBee  800 pts.

You also receive 1000 pts. for completing each level.

SnowBees don't move at full speed until level 10 is reached.

My current maximum score is Level 24 and 36500 points.
