 [Outline of application]
    - BALLOON is a game in which the player tries to smash balloons
      by having a pierrot jump.


 [Outline of the game]
    - The stage is cleared when the player successfully smashed the balloons.
      Then, the companions of the circus appear.

    - The flow of the game is as follows.
      *------------------------*
      |     Game beginning     |
      *------------------------*
                   |            
      *------------------------*
      |         stage1         |
      *------------------------*
                   |            
                   |Clear       
                   |            
      *------------------------*
      |         stage2         |
      *------------------------*
                   |            
                   |Clear       
                   |            
      *------------------------*
      |         stage3         |
      *------------------------*
                   |            
                   |Clear       
                   |            
      *------------------------*
      |  All stages clear      |
      *------------------------*


    - Method to clear stage 
      The player proceed to the next stage by successfully smash 3 balloons
      moving right and left on the appear part on the screen.
      (One point is added fir each balloon smashed)
      If missed, one point reduces from the player's point.
      (No deduction when the point is 0.)


 [Install]
    Please install by either of the following methods.

    [Installation from PC to PSION Series5]
      Double click BALLOON.SIS from PC and install according to the installer.

    [BALLOON.SIS is copied from PC to PSION Series5 and installs.]
      1) start "Add/remove" from "Control panel" of PSION Series5
      2) click "Add new"
      3) select BALLOON.SIS
      4) click "OK"
      Or, start BALLOON.SIS directly.
      Please specify where to install after checking the empty RAM(About 130KB)
      size of the machine.


 [UnInstall]
      1) start "Add/remove" from "Control panel" of PSION Series5
      2) select "BALLOON 1.00"
      3) click "Remove"


 [How to play]

    [Start]
    - Start the application by clicking "BALLOON" from - "Extras".


    [Operation]
    - Menu bar
        [File menu]
        - Exit :End the application.

        [Option menu]
        - Level Select:The level of the game can be selected from Easy
                       and Hard.
                       Default is Hard.
                       In Easy, the movement speed of the balloon slows.
                       Level can be changed while playing a game.
        - Auto Mode   :Start the Auto mode.


    [Stages]
      - The pierrot jumps to smash the balloons following
        on the upper part of the screen.
      - In the stage 2 and 3, a player can move the pierrot
        to the right and left.
      - In the stage 3, the balloon moves randomly.

        Clear condition :When three balloons are smashed, the stage is cleared.
        Operation method:Use the Enter Key or the cursor key
                         to have the pierrot jump.
                         The height of the jump depends on the power.
                         Press the Enter key after each jump
                         so that the pierrot is ready for the next jump.
        Game level      :The speed of the balloon is slower in the Easy than Hard.


    [About game over]
       There is no "Game Over".
