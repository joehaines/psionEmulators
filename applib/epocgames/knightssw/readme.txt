Knights - Shareware version
===========================

Thanks for downloading Knights, a fascinating puzzle game for the
Psion Series 5.

Installing Knights
------------------

By installing the software, you agree to the terms and conditions
layed out in the license agreement.

If you've installed Psion programs before, then you'll be familiar
with the process, and the only piece of information you need is as
follows:

Installation directory: C:\System\Apps\Knights

If you haven't installed Psion programs before, then follow these
instructions:

1) Connect your Psion to your PC in the normal way with PsiWin

2) From either your PC or Psion, goto the 'System\Apps' directory and
create a new directory called 'Knights'. If you can't see your system
directory, then on your Psion close any open directories, bring up
'Preferences' from the 'Tools' menu, and tick 'Show 'System' folder'.

3) From your PC, after unzipping the files, copy the following files
into the new directory you've just created:

KNIGHTS.AIF
KNIGHTS.APP
KPIC.MBM
KHS

If you bring up the extras bar the Knights icon should now be
available and the software is sucessfully installed. If you have any
difficulties installing Knights, please contact me on the following
e-mail address, explaining exactly what is going wrong.

E-mail: phil_stewart@yahoo.com
