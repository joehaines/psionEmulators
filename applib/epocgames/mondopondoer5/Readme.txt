What is MondoPondo? 
 
MondoPondo is a game, the best game in the world.  We really believe that. 
 
The most basic interpretation is that MondoPondo is a quiz game which can be played by anything from one to nine people.  It’s a great quiz game too, with thousands of questions and a natural language interface that talks and jokes with you, at you and about you. 
 
In its deepest incarnation MondoPondo is a quiz game that bites back with shockingly compromising Dares and soul-clawing Truths.  All of this  while still providing more fun than a drug fuelled trip to Disneyland with a group of the most fun people in the world. 
 
MondoPondo is modular.  This means you can download extra Dares, Truths and Questions to suit your needs.  (It already has thousands more questions than Trivial Pursuit and thousands of Dares and Truths).
 
From a game to play with your family and friends at Christmas (with children over 16) to a game to play in bed with your wife, husband or partner, MondoPondo delivers more than a post woman qualified in midwifery . 
 
MondoPondo is the best game in the world and we stand by that claim.  Try it and you'll see why.
