1 Presentation
-------------------


NeuroMax is a simple logic game. The purpose is to find the combination of operators(+, -, *, ÷) and given numbers to find the specified result. The problem is due to the limited amount of time allowed to resolve the answer.

For example, you have the following numbers : 9, 8, 7, 6, 5, 4
and you must find 999

One of the solutions is :
9 * 7 = 63
5 + 4 = 9
6 * 8 = 48
48 + 63 = 111
111 * 9 = 999




2 Installation
-----------------


NeuroMax is distributed in a SIS. To install it from your PC, you must have Psiwin 2.x or more.

To install it on your Epoc device, simply open NeuroMax.sis and follow the instructions. Contact me if you have a problem.



3 Shareware
-----------------


NeuroMax is a shareware (50 Fr or 7.62 Euros), if you want to have the complete version, you must register. Once registered, you will have the following options :

- A scores table containing best scores and the number of successful games for each score.
- The option to delete the scores table
- The option to see the solution of a problem you didn't manage to solve
- The option to obtain the solution of any problem just by giving the numbers and the result you want. The solution will appear in less than a second.
