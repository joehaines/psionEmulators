RockFall v1.05
==============

Overview
--------

RockFall is based on that game that (almost) everyone remembers... Boulder Dash.
In this version, you have to guide your trusty robot around thirty three different
mines collecting all the gems along the way. There are many puzzles packed into
these mines which should provide hours of entertainment. I'm sorry folks, but its
only available for the EPOC32 operating system (Series5 and GEOFOX).

Installation
------------

As you are reading this, I am assuming that you have unzipped RockFall, or your
version has been distributed in an uncompressed form.

However, you should now be able to see three files:

pe_rf105.sis  - RockFall installation
Sysram1.sis   - An upgrade provided by Psion
Readme.txt    - This file

If you have PsiWin version 2.1 then you should be covered and can click on the
pe_rf105.sis from either your PC or the Psion. This will start up the EPOC installer
program and the rest is easy. However, if you do not have PsiWin 2.1 then you need
to get your hands on the "Add remove control panel" add-on. This can be obtained
from Psion free of charge and you can find a link on my web site under the title
"If you have downloaded RockFall and cannot install it then read below!".

Basically you copy this file to your Psion and double click it... thus adding the
"Add remove control panel" add-on to your Psion. However, if you are an information
junkie like myself and want to browse through the official manual on the program that
you may want to look at the other link on my page.

When you have added the program mentioned above, you can copy the RockFall.sis file
onto your Psion and double click it, starting the installation process.

Troubleshooting
---------------

If you need any help installing RockFall, take a look at the RockFall web site,
http://www.planetepoc.com/rockfall/ or send an email to rockfall@planetepoc.com

When you have installed RockFall, take a look at the on-line help (CTRL+SHIFT+H)
