Usage:

Quake measures vibration and seismic activity by use of the Kurtsgel-Hussdorf Motion Differential. This algorhytham is used by Sesimic Survey institutions arround the world as well as on the Mars pathfinder missions and the the majority of pertrochemical survey expeditions.


The interface is simple to use. Hold the Psion`s pen tip inside the target area and wait for an earthquake. The severity of this of this quake is given in the real-time graph to the left of the screen. The callibration is in the Reictus Scale (Note: this standard scale is logarhythmic). 

It is important that the pen does not leave the target pad as this will create measurement innacuracies.


If there is no earthquake happening then you can test the precision on public transport such as trains, tubes, busses, coaches and even aircraft if  the relevant plane is travelling through a region of atmospheric turbulance.
