Race Form Cracker
---------------------

Race Form Cracker is designed to help you to identify the horse
with the best chance of winning in any race. You can use it to
analyse the huge amount of information available - rider and
trainer skills, weight carried, race conditions, horse's age,
form and fitness, and so on.

By processing this information using its powerul analysis
routines, Race Form Cracker will show you the horse with the
best chance of winning.

Each runner's rating - a number - is displayed and the higher
the number the more likely the horse is to win the race. The
most highly rated horse is highlighted.

Race information can be loaded and saved, any number of horses
can be entered and all the information you require is available
in daily newspapers. (No specialist race papers are required.)

You don't need to know anything about horse racing to use the
program, making it ideal for novices. A little knowledge through,
will enable you to improve the prediction routines by tweaking
the data entered.

Installation:
--------------
Unzip raceform.zip to extract the files. On your Psion series 5,
go to the c:\system\apps folder and create a folder called
RaceForm. (Select Tools, Preferences, Show system Folder if
necessary.) Copy all the files from the .zip to the
c:\System\Apps\RaceForm folder on the Psion. 50k of space is
required.

RaceForm Cracker will now appear on the Extras list.
