                   1.  Introduction

 Hearts is a card game for the Psion Series 5. Other people
might know the game by its other names, Black Maria or 
Chase the Lady.


                     2. Installing

 To install, drag the complete unzipped Hearts directory onto
the system/apps directory on any drive. The programs icon
should then appear on the extras bar.

                         Upgrading

 To upgrade from v1.0 just replace Hearts.app, logo.mbm and
cards.mbm files. Also you MUST delete the file Heartsdat from
the System/Apps/Hearts directory on your machine. Registered
users will need to re-enter their registration codes again. 

                         3.  Playing

 The  object of the game is to score as FEW points as possible.
When one of the players scores over 100, the game is over.
There are four players in the game. Three are controlled by
the Psion, the other by a human player.

 The game is started by the player with the 2 of clubs. The
player with this card must lay it. Each other player then
takes a turn until four cards are laid. Each player must
follow suit if possible. If they can't then they can lay any
card they like. When each player has laid a card the computer 
checks to see who won the 'trick'. The trick is
won by the player who followed suit with the highest card.
The computer then checks to see if any points were in the
trick and scores accordingly. Points are gained when a trick
is won that contains penalty cards. These are as follows..

All Hearts             Scores 1 point each.

Queen of Spades        Scores 13 points

All aces are high.
 
 As each hand progresses, players may not be able to follow
suit. This is their opportunity to discard the penalty cards
in their hand. That is why higher cards are best laid early
in the game. The skill is knowing what you can get away
with laying without incurring points.

 If your cards are all high, it might be worth trying to
'shoot the moon'. This means trying to win ALL the penalty
cards. If you succeed, all other players score the full 26
points and you score 0 points. If you fail though, you can
end up a loser!

 Play continues until all the cards are played. If someone
has scored over 100 points the game is over, otherwise the
computer deals another hand.

 At the start of each hand each player is given 13 cards.
Before actual play, each player must select three cards to
pass to the next player. This is when you can get rid of some
of the really nasty high cards. Beware though, one of the
computer hands will pass three cards on to you! The queen
of spades though cannot be passed on.
 
 When it is your turn to play an arrow will appear above
your cards. Just use the cursor keys  and the enter key to
select which card to play. Alternatively, the whole game
can be played using the pen. Just touch the card you wish
to lay. If the game asks you to press a key, simply touch
the screen anywhere to continue.

 Before the game, you might like to give each player a more
familiar name. Just select edit from the menu to change
the existing names. These name are remembered, and will
then appear whenever the game is re-run. Most of the other
menus are self explanatory.


  4.  Differences between registered and unregistered games

 With the Unregistered version of the game, when the human 
player wins the queen of spades they score 75 points instead 
of 13. Also it is not possible to 'shoot the moon'. The player
scores the full value of all penalty cards.
