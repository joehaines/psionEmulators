STB 1.0 (C) UK 1994-1997 Steve Litchfield
=======================================================
Released as freeware June 1997 ...

STB update information and new versions can be found at
http://3lib.ukonline.co.uk/

Requires Psion Series 5

STB is a new version of the old Pub favourite "Shut The Box", using
the Psion's abilities to add sound and graphics, plus do all the
counting for you!

It's an educational tool for children as it requires them to do quick
mental arithmetic to operate the game. It's also fun to play for adults,
who will appreciate more of the strategic and risk-taking elements.

For playing instructions and rules, please read the on-line help screens
that are built into the game!

Installation.
----------------------------
Just make a folder within the system area (you may need to turn on
viewing of the normally 'hidden' system area in your system screen
preferences), i.e.

\SYSTEM\APPS\STB

and put all the files in there. If all is well, the STB icon will appear
in your Extras bar.
