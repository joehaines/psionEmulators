INSTALLATION:
-------------

Yo-yo100.zip contains one sis file (yo-yo.sis)

You may instal to either Drive C or Drive D safely.

To install a .SIS file:

ON YOUR PALMTOP
 Copy the sis file to anywhere on your EPOC machine, and then  double tap each in turn.

ON YOUR PC
 Connect your EPOC machine and double click on the sis file.
