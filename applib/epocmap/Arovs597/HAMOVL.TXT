HAMOVL.TXT

A collection of amateur radio overlays for the
Psion series 5 Psi-MAPPER program

MAPPER was written by Steve Litchfield slitchfield@cix.compulink.co.uk

Installation.
------------
Put the *.MP3 and *.MBM files into any \System\Apps\MapperGB  folder.

BBSF2D.MP3   Packet Radio BBSs
REPT144.MP3  2m Repeaters
REPT433.MP3  70cm Repeaters
REPT50.MP3   Proposed 6m repeaters
REPTATV.MP3
REPTMW.MP3
BEACUK.MP3  50Mhz and above UK beacons.

Errors and omissions to  carl@ambrosia.demon.co.uk
