S5ANCIEN.ZIP

Set of 9 overlays for Steve Litchfields Psi-Mapper/GB elsewhere on this forum.
The overlays and .MBM files should be placed in the \System\Apps\MapperGB
folder.

The monuments in these overlays are open to the public or might be viewed from
the road. (Haven't visited them all so beware of surprises !)

Some of them are on farm land. Act like a guest when you're accessing private
grounds. Some (less spectaculair) monuments are sometimes hard to find, you
will need a good map and sometimes even a compass !
