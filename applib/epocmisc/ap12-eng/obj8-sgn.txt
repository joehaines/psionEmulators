JUPITER IN ARIES
The enthusiastic, faithful, wise, expansive, spontaneous sides of your nature are 
forceful, energetic, direct, courageous, and seek adventure and challenge.
*
JUPITER IN TAURUS
The enthusiastic, faithful, wise, expansive, spontaneous sides of your nature are 
practical, often skeptical and stubborn, and in search for serenity and inner peace.
*
JUPITER IN GEMINI
The enthusiastic, faithful, wise, expansive, spontaneous sides of your nature are 
inquisitive, witty, perceptive, adaptable, and seek out information.
*
JUPITER IN CANCER
The enthusiastic, faithful, wise, expansive, spontaneous sides of your nature are not 
so well developed, and you are more tempted into introspective, emotional, and 
protective environments.
*
JUPITER IN LEO
The enthusiastic, faithful, wise, expansive, spontaneous sides of your nature are 
proud, gregarious, dramatic, dignified, and desire self expression.
*
JUPITER IN VIRGO
The enthusiastic, faithful, wise, expansive, spontaneous sides of your nature are 
analytical, critical, modest, helpful, and work toward perfection.
*
JUPITER IN LIBRA
The enthusiastic, faithful, wise, expansive, spontaneous sides of your nature are 
affectionate, tolerant, often indecisive, tend to beauty, and seek balance, relationship, 
and calmness.
*
JUPITER IN SCORPIO
The enthusiastic, faithful, wise, expansive, spontaneous sides of your nature are in 
contradiction with your penetrating, suspicious, and introspective tendencies.
*
JUPITER IN SAGITTARIUS
The enthusiastic, faithful, wise, expansive, spontaneous sides of your nature are 
jovial, open minded, tend to freedom, and seek meaning and new experience.
*
JUPITER IN CAPRICORNUS
The enthusiastic, faithful, wise, expansive, spontaneous sides of your nature are 
more industrious, practical, and disciplined than really expressed in relations with 
others.
*
JUPITER IN AQUARIUS
The enthusiastic, faithful, wise, expansive, spontaneous sides of your nature are 
progressive, erratic, revolutionary, idealistic, humanitarian, inventive, and desire 
individuality.
*
JUPITER IN PISCES
The enthusiastic, faithful, wise, expansive, spontaneous sides of your nature are 
imaginative and impressionable. You like others more for listening than speaking.
