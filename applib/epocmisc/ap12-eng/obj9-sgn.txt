SATURN IN ARIES
The part of your mind that is disciplined, respectful, and solitary is forceful, energetic, 
direct, courageous, and seek adventure and challenge.
*
SATURN IN TAURUS
The part of your mind that is disciplined, respectful, and solitary is practical, often 
skeptical and stubborn, and in search for serenity and inner peace.
*
SATURN IN GEMINI
The part of your mind that is disciplined, respectful, and solitary is inquisitive, witty, 
perceptive, adaptable, and seeks out information.
*
SATURN IN CANCER
The part of your mind that is disciplined, respectful, and solitary is introspective, 
emotional, protective, and wants inner and outer security.
*
SATURN IN LEO
The part of your mind that is disciplined, respectful, and solitary is proud, gregarious, 
dramatic, dignified, and desires self expression.
*
SATURN IN VIRGO
The part of your mind that is disciplined, respectful, and solitary is analytical, critical, 
modest, helpful, and works toward perfection.
*
SATURN IN LIBRA
The part of your mind that is disciplined, respectful, and solitary is affectionate, 
tolerant, often indecisive, tends to beauty, and seeks balance, relationship, and 
calmness.
*
SATURN IN SCORPIO
The part of your mind that is disciplined, respectful, and solitary is penetrating, 
suspicious, introspective, and desires to transform and remove outer masks.
*
SATURN IN SAGITTARIUS
The part of your mind that is disciplined, respectful, and solitary would like to be 
jovial, open minded, tending to freedom, and seeking meaning and new experience.
*
SATURN IN CAPRICORNUS
The part of your mind that is disciplined, respectful, and solitary is industrious, 
practical, and works for solitude and personal integrity.
*
SATURN IN AQUARIUS
The part of your mind that is disciplined, respectful, and solitary is progressive, 
erratic, revolutionary, idealistic, humanitarian, inventive, and desires individuality.
*
SATURN IN PISCES
The part of your mind that is disciplined, respectful, and solitary is imaginative, other 
worldly, impressionable, and seeks transcendence of self.
