Installing Business Warrior :
-----------------------------
1) Connect your EPOC device to your desktop
2) Double click on BW.SIS

Please select your preferences after launching BW and read the help file.

Earlier Beta Version :
---------------------
The old database isn't compatible with this version of Business Warrior. Sorry for this trouble.
