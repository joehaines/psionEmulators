Introduction
Calendar Notes is the Freeware 'easy to use' agenda program for Psion computers and most compatibles.
Aimed at users who need an agenda, but do not require the complexity of Psion Agenda as built into your machine.
Calendar Notes enables you to manage your day-to-day To-do's, reminders, appointments and general day-notes in a simple one screen layout with a monthly calendar. 
Yearly UK calendar events such as New Year's Day and Easter Sunday are supported with the use of text displayed alongside the date to which it refers - just like a real calendar!
Learning to use Calendar Notes will take just a few minutes of your time.

Installation details
Copy the Calndr30 folder to your EPOC palmtop.
If you already have a Add/remove control panel on your computer, simply double tap on the Calendar.sis file. This will start the automatic installation of Calendar Notes.
NOTE: If you do not have a Add/remove control panel on your EPOC palmtop, copy the instexe.exe file direct to your palmtop computer (it's on the Palmtop CD).  Open instexe.exe - it will install the control panel and then delete itself.

Hope you find Calendar Notes a useful addition to your palmtop computer.
