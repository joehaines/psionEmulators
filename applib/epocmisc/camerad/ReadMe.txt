Introduction

Camerad is a program mainly for Camera Assistants,
but might also be of use to DPs and Photographers. 
Camerad is designed for use on the Psion 5 handheld 
computer.

Camerad consists of various modules that let you 
calculate Depth of Field, calculate an f-stop for 
a given Depth of Field, convert footage to time, 
calculate time lapses, time remaining footage and 
do some more tasks.

Camerad likes both the metric and imperial system. 
Camerad has a smart rounding feature and displays 
f-stops in a practical way. Camerad utilises the 
touch screen abilities of the Psion 5. Camerad 
remembers your settings after closing the program.

Camerad has been written by a Camera Assistant, 
and was programmed completely on the Psion 5.
