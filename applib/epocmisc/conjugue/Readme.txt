I Presentation

Conjugue is a powerful software which enable you to conjugate any french verb to the most used tenses. The software know every irregular verbs, it cans give you the model used to conjugate the verb you entered. This software is also really fast, the conjugation takes en instant. The spell checking allow you to check the good spelling of the verb entered (if you have the french dictionnary) and offer you alternatives if the spelling is wrong. Conjugue is also simple to use, enter the French infinitive of the verb and press <Enter>, that's all.



II installation

To install Conjugue, Run the Conjugue.sis file on your PC or your Epoc machine and follow the instructions.



III Shareware

Conjugue is a shareware, it costs 100 Fr.You can use during an unlimited period but only a few tenses are available for conjugation in the unregistered version.
