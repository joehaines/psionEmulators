It has been release for evaluation purpose for 21 days.

This version is compatible with PSION SERIES 5 / 5mx.

========================
To install
========================

1) Connect your Psion Series 5 with your PC using EPOC Connect and double click on ETools.SIS file, This will start installation.

2) Follow on screen instruction.
