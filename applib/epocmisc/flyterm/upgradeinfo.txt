WARNING
========

When installing this software on a PSION Series 5, or Geofox where
 a previous version of flyTERM (before Version v1.04) is installed, it is
very important that the previous version is removed before installing
flyTERM V1.04 or above.

To remove the software klick the following:

-Control Panel-
     -Add/Remove- 
         -flyTERM Terminal Emulation-
             -Remove

In the case of problems please send an e-mail to:

           info@flyingdata.de

for assistance.
