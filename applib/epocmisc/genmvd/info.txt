INFO.TXT: File sizes
====================

This file lists the sizes (in bytes) of the GenMVD and Ged2Gen files that you
have installed on your Psion. They are included to provide an additional check
that everything has been downloaded an installed correctly. Note that these are
file sizes taken from the Psion: you must compare them with the files on your
Psion not your desktop machine!

(this information is for this release of GenMVD 2.00 and Ged2Gen 2.01 only)

   GED2GEN\

      WIN.TBL         283 bytes

      GENMVD.HLP     4932 bytes
