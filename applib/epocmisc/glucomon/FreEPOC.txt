- About FreEPOC

FreEPOC is an EPOC software house with a difference. While every one of our products is of commercial quality, they are not crippled in any way. You are not forced to pay for them. They are not shareware.

But don’t think that means they are in any way not as good as their commercial counterparts.

FreEPOC sustains itself on donations from many satisfied users - we couldn’t have got to where we are today without their generosity. If you want to help us, details are on our website (www.freepoc.org).
