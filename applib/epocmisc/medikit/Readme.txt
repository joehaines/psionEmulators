MediKit v3.01

Medical clinical assistant and calculator for the EPOC range of 
handheld computers.


Installation Instructions
-------------------------
1. Unzip the zipfile to your PC or your EPOC machine

2. a. On your PC, double click the MediKit.sis file and installation 
      should take place on your EPOC machine.

   or

   b. On your EPOC machine, double the tap the MediKit.sis file and 
      MediKit installation should result in the MediKit icon appearing 
      on your 'Extras Bar'.


History
-------
Please see the file `History.S5` (on your Psion) for details of changes 
to MediKit in this version.


Running the program
-------------------
-  Press the 'Extras' button on the toolbar, and click the 'MediKit' icon.
-  When presented with the main MediKit screen, press the 'Menu' button or 
   click the screen and select from the choices presented.	


Operating Instructions
----------------------
Read the help file (select `Help` from the main menu) for further information.
