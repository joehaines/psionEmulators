
PSILOG V1.00 - 27/03/2001
=========================

Thank you for downloading PsiLog.  The program is freeware; you download, install and use it at your own risk.

PsiLog is a programme for radio and TV reporters and producers who want to log live events, e.g. sporting events or news conferences in real time.  In effect, it is a clock showing time-of-day that can be stopped and read so that the event can subsequently be found on a recording of the event.

PsiLog currently runs only on the Psion Series 5.

Later versions will be written to run on other EPOC machines and will include the ability to type the notes of the event on the machine itself.


FILES IN PSILOG.ZIP
===================

PSILOG.SIS - The EPOC installation file.
README.TXT - This file.


INSTALLATION
============

Unzip PSILOG.ZIP to extract PSILOG.SIS either to a temporary folder on your PC or on your Psion.

Install in the conventional manner, either by connecting your Psion to your PC and double-clicking on PSILOG.SIS or by tapping PSILOG.SIS on your Psion.
