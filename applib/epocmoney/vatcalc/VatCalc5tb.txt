VatCalc5tb , a simple program which calculates the Vat to add to a price ,
or the Vat content of an existing ( Vat inclusive ) price .

To install Vatcalc5tb simply double click on the SIS file , or copy the SIS file
to your Psion and use the Add/Remove command in the control panel to add
the program .

Start Vatcalc5tb from the Extras bar , You will be asked to select a conversion
the options are Plus Vat , Minus Vat , Exit ( and when not run on a Revo , Set 
Rate ) . If being used on a Revo and the Vat rate is increased , you can set 
the new rate to use by pressing Ctrl+R . Otherwise operation is fairly obvious in
use , enter the appropriate price in the relevant place , press enter and the
results will be displayed . Note that the 'Exit' button and command ( Ctrl+E )
will NOT work if any dialog is being displayed .

Vatcalc5tb is freeware , I use it on my Revo reglarly
and I have not suffered from any unusual growths,
injuries or data loss as a result . 
I cannot promise that you won't either .
