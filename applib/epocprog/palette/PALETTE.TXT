                          EPOC5 256 colour Palette
                         Version 1.00        7/6/99

Symbian have stated that the 16 colour mode of EPOC release 5 uses the EGA
colours, but haven't mentioned what colours the 256 colour mode uses. This
archive contains a JASC palette of the 256 colours, together with a bitmap
showing all the colours. They were obtained by drwaing a large amount of
squares on the emulator, and are, as far as I can be certain, the colours
used by the fixed 256 colour modes.

They are for use for any programmers converting to EPOC 5, who want to
know what colours they can use without dithering.

comments or suggestions.
