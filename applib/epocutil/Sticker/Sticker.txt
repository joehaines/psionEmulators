MMM Sticker v2.7

--
Copy All files exclude this file into "\SYSTEM\APPS\MMM Sticker"
--

This software mainly purpose is use as a memo sticker for your PSION S5.

When you turn-on your machine, it will go to foreground, and display all your
Memo Sticker(s), auto go to background, and able to switch on backlight 
and play power-on music.

[Fn]+[N] : Hot-Key to switch to MMM Sticker

Preference setting will be saved while exit program.
