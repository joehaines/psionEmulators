Charmap is freeware programmers tool - Use and distribute freely.


CharMap is a simple ASCII Character Map for the Psion Series 5.
It is Pen compatible, and you can use the copy/paste button on the screen
to copy the highlighted character to the clipboard.


Hotkeys:
--------

. arrow keys, PageUp,PageDown,Home,End to move through table
. Space bar to enter a character number
. Typing any character will jump to that character



Installation:
-------------

Double click on the Charmap.sis file to install. The following files are 
installed.

Charmap.app	\System\Apps\Charmap
Charmap.aif	\System\Apps\Charmap
