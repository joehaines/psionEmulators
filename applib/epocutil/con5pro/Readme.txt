This version is compatible with PSION SERIES 5 / 5mx and GEOFOX 1.

========================
To install
========================

1) Connect your Psion Series 5 with your PC using EPOC Connect and double click on Con5Pro.SIS file, This will start installation.

2) Follow on screen instruction.
