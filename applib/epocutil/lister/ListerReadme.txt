*** ReadMe file for Lister ***

Lister is a Macro5 compatible macro that stores selected text
strings for later retrieval. Lister cannot function without a
proper macro program. Suggested macro starters are ExAb,
Assistant, Macro5, Extrabars, Merlin and Hotkeys.
Copy Lister.OPO to the folder containing your macros. Usually,
this is C:\Macros or D:\Macros. With ExAb (v. 7.0 or higher) run
Lister after choosing it from the list with macros after <Menu+J>
or <Menu+P>. For the other macro programs: assign a hotkey to
Lister and make sure the macros folder is correctly entered in
the macro program's settings.

Enjoy Lister
