SetScreen v1.05
---------------

The Series 5 screen contrast is very sensitive
to the temperature. SetScreen allows you to
quickly adjust the contrast to its optimized
value, when the 16 gray colors are distinct
and the graphics have their intended shades.

SetScreen is also available as a macro for Macro5.


Installation:
-------------
1 - Install the package FreeMem.sis as you do with
    any SIS file : double-click on it either on
    the PC or on the Series 5.
2 - If you don't already have it, install also SystInfo.sis


Usage:
------
Use the arrows to adjust the contrast. It is fine when you
can only see big squares of 2 colors.

You can also directly use the icon of SetScreen in the
Extras-bar with the standard contrast+ and contrast- keys.
