Simplicity for EPOC (Series 5/mx, Ericcson)

To install, put the Simplicity folder into System/Apps on your Psion.

The folder "Bits" contains some backdrops for Simplicity, they are not
critical to the use of Simplicity, but if you have room on your Psion, you may
as well put them wherever you want them (Should be in Documents and not System
though).
