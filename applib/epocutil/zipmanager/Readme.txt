INSTALLATION

There are two ways to install ZIP Manager on your device. The first way:

1. Download the ZIPManager.zip file to your PC.
2. Unpack the Zip file contents, and double click on ZIPManager.SIS file.
3. Follow the instructions which will guide you through the installation process.

The second way:

1. Download the ZIPManager.zip file to your PC.
2. Unpack the Zip file contents.
3. Upload ZIPManager.SIS file on your device.
4. Double click on ZIPManager.SIS file there.
5. Follow the instructions which will guide you through the installation process.

DESCRIPTION

An Ultimate Compression Tool for Your Psion! Reduce your memory needs by 50%!

ZIP Manager is the ultimate archive/compression tool for your Psion. ZIP Manager
provides effective and fast zip compression and de-compression on your Psion.

* It lets you unzip, directly on your Psion, files and programs
  downloaded from the Internet   in ZIP format. This is an big
  convenience factor and an important "standalone" capability
  for your Psion.

* It lets you to produce your own ZIP files for archiving infrequently
  used files and programs to save free space on your Psion.

* It is fully compatible with PKZip, Zip and WinZip software for DOS and Windows.

ZIP Manager easily cuts your memory needs in half by zippping all your
infrequently used files and programs.

ZIP Manager is a "must have" program for every Psion owner who is concerned
about limited memory.
