MM version 3.0, 02/11/97, Ad Bosch
Company / Personal promotion
desktop clock / year 2000 count down timer

MM is a desktop clock which can be configured to
display your company logo, slogan and name 
(or photographs, etc.). It has no particular function
except telling the time informing about the time left 
till Annum MM. It does however show you some of the 
abilities of your S5 and of OPL.

Features:
Year 2000 countdown timer
Clock, day & date
Night/day indication
Game Noughts & Crosses
3 animated images:
- logo (could be your company!!!)
- dancer
- girls
Switch image, enlarge/reduce image & freeze options
And it all moves, jumps, dances, rotates...

Install: all files under directory SYSTEM\APPS\MM\
(drive C: or D:)

Use: 
<Tab> switches image
<Menu> allows:	play game
					chime on/off
					large/reduced image size
					switch image
					company/holder info
					program info
					quit
<Space> freezes image; repeat <space>: next image ; any other key
	releases
<Esc> quits
 
MM was picked up, grabbed, cut, copied, pasted,
