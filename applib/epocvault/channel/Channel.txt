Channel is a very simple program . All it does is convert a given UHF channel number
into its vision carrier frequency or from frequency to channel number .

To install Channel simply double click on the SIS file , or copy the SIS file
to your Psion and use the Add/Remove command in the control panel to add
the program .

To use Channel , select it from the Extras Bar . The dialog will ask you which
type of conversion you would like ( Channel - from channel number to frequency )
or ( Frequency - from frequency to channel number ) . When entering the
Frequency you do not need to be precise , the program will automatically convert
to the nearest channel number . If you need to know the exact frequency - convert
the channel number back into a frequency and the exact Vision Carrier Frequency
will be calculated .
