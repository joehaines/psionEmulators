F-Plot 1.0
----------

Download & Installation

1. Download FPlot10b.zip
2. Create a folder "FPlot" on your PC
3. Extract the FPlot10 file into this folder
4. Check contents of folder:
        FPlot.aif  (1k)
        FPlot.app  (5k)
        FPlot.hlp  (4k)
        Readme.txt (1k)
5. Move the text files into another folder on your PC for later reading
5. Move the "FPlot" folder to your Psion: c:/system/apps/FPlot
6. Start FPlot from your Extra bar (press ESC to close the intro frame)
7. Send me an e-mail to confirm downloading (hempelget.uni-paderborn.de)(Thanks!)
