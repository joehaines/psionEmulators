Locators for Psion Series 5 and Geofox

This is a translation of my Psion 3a locator program, rearranged to run
on the Psion series 5 and Geofox.

It deals with conversions between IARU Maidenhead locators, QRA locators,
and UK National Grid references. It calculates distances between any of
these three.

It understands the NGR used in the Channel Islands (Guernsey, etc.) and
Northern Ireland.

Copy locators.opo to any folder from where it can be run by double tapping
it (or highlight and press Enter).
