Mailing Lists

A mailing list has been created for announcements and discussions concerning PsiFS. To join or remove yourself from the list email majordomo_server@btinternet.com with one of the following commands in the message body:
help
Obtain a summary of the supported commands.
info psifs_list
Retrieve a description of the PsiFS mailing list.
list
List all the current mailing lists on the server, whether they are moderated or closed, and whether they are secure.
subscribe psifs_list
Subscribe to the PsiFS mailing list.
unsubscribe psifs_list
Unsubscribe from the PsiFS mailing list.
Note: Commands must be place in the body of the message, not the subject.
