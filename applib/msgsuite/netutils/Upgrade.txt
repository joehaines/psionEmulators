THIS INFORMATION IS FOR UPGRADERS ONLY!

If you upgrade from the previous NetUtils version you may want to keep
your lists of WHOIS and NTP servers.

This is a good moment to do so:

1. Before the installation (!) copy files "whois.dat" and "ntp.dat"
from the folder where the NetUtils installed currently.
2. Install the new version
3. Manually replace new files "whois.dat" and "ntp.dat" by saved ones.

If you run installer now without having your server lists previously
saved they will be deleted and replaced by standard ones.
