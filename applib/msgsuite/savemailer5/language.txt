From version 2.51 onwards, the Setup procedure offers a choice of machine

with the default settings, with the following information I can easily upgrade

1)  Your machine model and language (e.g. Psion S5 mx - Italian)

    and time line)

... and the following shortcuts:

3)  in Email and Word: "select all" (e.g. "Ctrl+a" for UK/US machines)

7)  in Email: Save Attachments (e.g. "Ctrl+Shift+s" for UK/US)

    (e.g. "y" for UK/US)
9)  in Data: New Entry (e.g. "Ctrl+Shift+n" for UK/US)
10) in Data: Save Entry (e.g. "Ctrl+s" for UK/US)
