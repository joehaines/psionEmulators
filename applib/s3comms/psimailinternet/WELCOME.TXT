Welcome to the Psimail Internet Mail application. 
This is where you prepare and store all your email messages. You can send messages and files from your Psion to anyone with an Internet email account, and receive messages and files that others have sent you.

Before you send your first message, make sure that you have entered your Internet account details in the Setup application. You can set up a new PsionWorld account, or enter the details for an existing TCP/IP dial-in account held with another Internet service provider.
