Installing...
=============
Copy the following files to the following directorys on the same drive. Only
copy the .wve files if you want sound. (You can choose to have beeps instead)

File           Directory
====           =========

Ants.opa       \APP\
Game.opo       \ANTS\
Data.pic       \ANTS\
Levels.opd     \ANTS\
AntYip.wve     \ANTS\   (Optional)
AntScrm.wve    \ANTS\   (Optional)

Once you have copied the files to your Psion you must then place the
highlight at the position you wish to install the icon for Ants and press
Psion-I. In the dialog select the main Ants.opa file and press return. The
icon for Ants should then appear on your system screen. Placing the highlight
under the icon and pressing retun from now on should let you play Ants.

Instructions...
===============

If you have ever played the game Lemmings before then you should recognise
how to play the game, but for those of you who haven't basicly what happens
is your ants start in the level and they just keep walking. If they walk into
somthing they will turn around and if they fall onto some spikes they will
die.

Your ants can all do special jobs to help them get to the exit on the level
and all the jobs use equipment up depending on how much you use that job.

The different jobs...
=====================

        Dig
        ---
        Selecting this will make the ant walk to the end of the current 'tile'
        and start digging. The ants can't dig through walls.

        Tunnel
        ------
        Basicly the same as dig but going fowards instead!

        Bridge
        ------
        Selecting this option will make an ant bridge over gaps when it gets
        to them instead of falling down the gap.

        Block
        -----
        The ant will walk to the end of the current 'tile' and hold up a STOP
        sign making any ant that encounters it turn around.

        Explode
        -------
        The ant will walk to the end of the current tile and detonate the
        explosives it's carrying. This will blow a hole underneath it in
        anything including brick walls! (But the ant will die)

The aim of each level...
========================

Well, to get as many ants as possible to the exit - of course! (If more than
two ants die then you will be forced to play the level again)

Controls...
===========

Press 1-5 to select the ant to give orders to and press space to tell the ant
what to do. If you press space a dialogue will apear with the different jobs
on the left and the amount of equipment for each job on the right. If '--'
is written on the right that means there is no equipment left to do that job
any more. If the ant already has a job you will be given the choice to either
cancel or continue doing that job.
