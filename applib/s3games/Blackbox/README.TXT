2. OVERVIEW
   ~~~~~~~~
	BlackBox is a game of deduction. The experimenter fires
	electrons into the "Black Box" and tries to deduce the 
	whereabouts of the atoms based on where the electrons
	enter and exit the "Black Box". Sometimes electrons are
	deflected by one or more of the atoms, sometimes they are
	reflected, and sometimes the electron is absorbed by an
	atom.

	The object of the game is to correctly deduce the location
	of ALL of the atoms in the "Black Box" with as few electrons
	as possible. Incorrect guesses are penalised at the rate of 
	10 points for every atom you get incorrect. The penalty is
	applied each time you "try" a guess, so don't be too bold
	with your guesses.

4. INSTALLATION:
   ~~~~~~~~~~~~
	1) Copy BlackBox.opa to the \APP\ directory on any drive on
	   your 3a.
	2) (Optional) Copy BlackBox.rsc to an \APP\BLACKBOX\ 
	   directory on any drive of your 3a.
	3) Install the BlackBox.opa game using Psion-I from the
	   System screen.
