* Thanks for downloading Blades! *

This is very easy to install - 

Put BLADES.OPA in \APP\
Put LEVELS.DAT in \APP\BLADES\
Put MOWER.PIC in  \APP\BLADES\

Install BLADES.OPA from the system screen by pressing Psion-I and selecting
BLADES.OPA. Press Enter, and the Blades icon will appear.

Press Enter again, and you are ready to redefine the keys and start playing
Blades!

Full online help is available, and is reached via the main menu.
