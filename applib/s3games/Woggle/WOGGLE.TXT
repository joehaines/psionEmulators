Woggle
======

Freeware!

A game similar to Boggle, a game played by two or more people.

The object of the game is to find as many words as possible in a matrix of letters.  The original game consisted of a box of lettered dice which would fall into cavities after beeing shaken.  The letters on the dice are then read: up, down, right, left and diagonally to make as many words as possible in the time allowed (3 minutes).  Each dice may only be used once per word.

The winner is the player who manages to make as many words.

I have tried to emulate the dice version of the game as closely as possible by selecting letters not at random but biased in probability in a similar manner to the frequency of occurrence on the faces of the dice.  This is reasonably successful.
