Thank you for choosing Golf Kaddie 99, Version 1.0 for the Psion 3a, 3c and 3mx palmtop
computers.

Golf Kaddie 99 needs approximately 35Kb to install, and 90Kb to run.

Instalation of Golf Kaddie 99 is very easy:

1. Move golf99.opa into the /app/ directory of your Psion on any of its disks you choose. 

2. On the System Screen of your Psion, Select Menu, then Install, and select golf99.opa. 

3. The Golf Kaddie 99 icon ( a golf flag ) will appear in the System Screen. 

We strongly recommend you read the manual.txt & license.txt documents to familiarize
yourself with Golf Kaddie 99.

Please report any problems or bugs you find with Golf Kaddie 99 to geoff.inline@tpg.com.au

including information not contained here.
