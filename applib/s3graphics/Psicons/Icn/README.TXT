This is a collection of icon designs which I
have produced over the past 2 years, using
Rick Andrews excellent "Draw2" program (now up
to version 2.82).

May I also recommend Chris Netocny's "IconVu"
as the best app for viewing (and managing) my
"gallery".  Both these apps are available on
section 18 of the Compuserve Psion forum.

You will see that my designs are grouped in sub-
directories so as to make it easier for you to
find one of a particular type.  The main directory
is \ICN\ - which is where the Psion looks for
pics in making new "lists" or "groups" from the
"Special" menu of the system screen.

This work is issued as freeware, to promote
my availability for GUI design work - and to
plug the 2 apps mentioned above!
