HAMOVL.TXT


A collection of amateur radio overlays for the
Psion series 3a MAPPER program

MAPPER was written by Steve Litchfield slitchfield@cix.compulink.co.uk


Installation.

Put the *.MP3 and *.PIC files into any APP/MAPPER3A/  directory.

REPT433.MP3  70cm Repeaters
