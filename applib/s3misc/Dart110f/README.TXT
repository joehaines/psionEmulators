Introduction:
=============
Dart3a is a easy to use darts program,  which includes x01 and 
Tac-Tic.  You can not only  use it to keep  track of the score 
during a dart-game, but you can also play against the computer 
in case there is  no-one around to play against.  This way you 
can always play against an opponent of your own strength.

Additionally,  for x01,  darts3a can  help you to analyse your 
game,  by calculating  your average score,  the spread of your 
score and your succes percentage on checking.

Darts3a can  also show you a graphical  representation of your 
accomplishments.

Files in this archive:
======================
               dart3a.opa      application
               sys$help.opo    help-engine
               dart3a.hlp      helpfile
               readme.txt      this file
               history.txt     development info

Installation:
=============
1) Copy dart3a.opa   to an \app directory. 
2) Copy dart3a.hlp   to an \opd directory.
3) Copy sys$help.opo to an \opo directory.
    (not needed if this file already exist on your system)
4) Install the dart3a application in the system-screen.(psion-I)

If you're really short of disk-space you can skip steps
2 and 3, to install without online help.

Dart3a needs about 40k of free memory to run.
