                JWbox

JWbox is a macro written by John Boyce and Wolfgang Schirmer.
It allows you to use semigraphical characters in your word 
documents.

INSTALLATION
============
Just copy the JWbox.mco file into one of your \macro\mco
directories and install it from the macro main screen, assigning
a hotkey of your choice.

USAGE
=====
During the execution of the macros only a few keys are captured:

<enter>        works normal
<space>        works normal
<help)         shows a screen with the captured keys

7: Ú     8: Ä   9: ¿
u: À     i:  ³   o: Ù
j:  Ã    k:  Å   l: ´
m: Á            .: Â

those keys paint single line characters. 
Together with <shift> double line characters are painted.

<esc> or <psion>x terminate the macro.

The macro is also terminated if you leave word and go to another
application. This was necessary to avoid big problems with the 
redirected keys in other applications.

NOTE:
=====
Please leave the help-screen with <enter>, not with <esc>, because
<esc> will stop the macro.
