Paul,

You can disable clicks for the currently running OPL program with this 
procedure:

PROC click:(on%)
        local ax%,bx%,pad3%
        local pad4%,pad5%,pad6%
        ax%=$7e02
        if on%=0
          bx%=1
        endif
        os($8d,addr(ax%))
ENDP
