The following program can alter the contrast for you.

PROC contrast:
  local ax%,bx%,cx%,dx%,si%,di%   : rem keep together
  
  ax%=$1201
  os($8e,addr(ax%))
ENDP

set ax% to $12ff to decrease the contrast, and to $1201
to increase the contrast.
