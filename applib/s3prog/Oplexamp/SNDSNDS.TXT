The ring and chime alarms use the SND: driver to produce an
alarm note sequence. You can use the sounds as follows:

PROC alarm:
  global ret%,id%,alarm&

  alarm&=1

  ret%=ioopen(id%,"SND:",-1)
  iow(id%,9,alarm&, #0)
  get
  ioclose(id%)
ENDP

set alarm& to 0 for rings, and to 1 for chimes.
