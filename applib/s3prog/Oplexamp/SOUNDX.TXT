Re: playing DTMF from OPL
 
A better idea would be to re-enable the sound system before
you dial the number, and restore the sound system afterwards.
 
You can do this as follows (requires GLOBAL st%):
 
PROC sound:(e%)
 local ax%,bx%,cx%,dx%,si%,di%
 
 if e%
   st%=call($0f8b)         rem read sound flags into st%
   bx%=(st% and 31) or 4
 else
   bx%=st%
 endif
 
 ax%=$1000
 os($8b,addr(ax%))         rem set sound flags using bx%
ENDP
 
Using sound:(1) will store the current sound setting in
st% and enable the sound, and sound:(0) will restore the
sound setting to that in st%.
