Introduction
	Piano Helper is a small shareware utility to assist anyone playing the piano or keyboards.   The functions include:

- Chords: fingering for Major, Minor, Aug, Dim and 7th and the ability to
	      	transpose the notes up or down the keys.

- Scales: fingering for Major, Minor (3 types) and Blues

- Circle of 5ths: chart for handy music composition or if you are trying
		 to figure out a song by ear.

- Note Tutor: ability to have notes labelled for you if you are learning 
	      	them.

- Musical terms:  definitions, limited unregistered amount.  Full access
  		when you register.

Files included:
- Pianohlp.opa
- Pianohlp.*         (*.wrd, *.txt  -  this file in word and text format)
- Whatsnew.*      (History of program and improvements)




Installation
1) Copy Pianohlp.opa into   APP  directory on any drive
2) From system screen Install Pianohlp.opa  using  <PSION><I>
3) Enjoy!
