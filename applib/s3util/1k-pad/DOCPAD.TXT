1k-pad
Version 1.0  (01/1997)
Jean-Yves Pontailler

This small program is a freeware so you can copy it and distribute it (without modifying it). You can also rename it for your personal convinience.

1k-pad is a very simple notepad that only uses 1K of the internal memory of your Series 3a. It can be installed in any \OPO\ directory. The use of Flash disks causes no problem.

You can edit and save an unlimited number of notes up to 6 lines of 50 characters each. This notes are saved in a JTER.DAT file in the \OPD\ directory of the internal disk. When you launch 1k-pad, it is ready for typing a note. You can edit the current line only. You can't modify the notes but delete them only. All indications needed are displayed on the lower part of the screen.
