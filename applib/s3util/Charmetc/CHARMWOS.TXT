I wrote this Macro, because I often have to write text in spanish or catalan
and I tried to simulate the Insert Special Character in Word for Windows.

This Macro only works if you have the Macro V.2.0 and the Charm Application,
both from Tom Dolbilin.

THANKS TOM FOR THESE GREAT APPLICATIONS

Installation:

Put the charmwos.mco file into your \macro\mco\ Directory and install it into
a free slot. Assign a Hotkey to the macro.

Call the macro from any Application, that support the Bring Services. It will
jump to Charm. There you can select the special character you need. After that
you must press again your Hotkey combination to jump back to you original
Application.


Let me know all bugs and suggestions.
