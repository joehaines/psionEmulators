             Dbf2spr
             -------
             
             (c) 1994 M.C.Millar
             
             Dbf files are accepted as the input file, Spr files
             readable by Sheet are produced, if "Rows" is selected the
             records are written out, with the fields along the row.
             If "Columns" is selected then the records are written out
             in consecutive columns.
             
             Dbf2spr converts Dbf files, as created by Data, or opd
             files with all string fields.
             Fields in data are limited to 255 characters long, if they
             are over this and less than 1022 characters long they will
             be split into multiple cells. Fields over 1022 characters
             will be truncated.  Numerical fields are converted to
             strings.
