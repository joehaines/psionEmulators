SPR2DBF

This is the companion to DBF2SPR, it converts SPR files to DBF files.

However:

It is limited to 255 records in length.  If you need more than this
mail me at millar@cix.compulink.co.uk or 100021,3354 on Compuserve 
and I'll see what I can do.

It is limited to text records _ONLY_ formulas evaluating to text won't do, neither
will numbers.  If the record starts with " you should be OK.
