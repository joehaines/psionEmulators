   There is no need (I think)  of  instructions!  If you don't know Tetris at
   all, give a look to the on-line help system (available even while  playing
   by pressing the Help key) or simply ask to a friend of yours.

   I  made  no pause key, but the game enters in a pause mode when you change
   the active process (pressing  the  System  key,  for  example) or when you
   press the Menu key.

   Remember that, in order to access to the  menu  functions,  you  must  not
   press  the  hot-key  itself,  but you must press the Menu key then the hot
   key, instead.
