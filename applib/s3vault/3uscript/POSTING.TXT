3USCRIPT is a collection of scripts to extend the facilities of ReadCIS,
the Psion 3a Compuserve Offline Reader. 3USCRIPT provides retrieval of
Compuserve address book, retrieval of file listings from forums, retrieval
of UK TV listings and, new in version 2, retrieval of a report of recent 
online charges. Version 2 also supports changes made on Compuserve since version
1 was released, better reliability, a new setup program, and a new online Guide.
