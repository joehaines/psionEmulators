BATTERY CHECKER v1.3

This program gives you a graphic representation of the condition of your
batteries. This includes...

        Main Batteries
        Lithium
        RAM SSD Batteries
        External power
        3Fax Batteries

It will also tell you if you have enough battery voltage to write to FLASH,
or to hear Sound. This can be very helpful.

NEW IN 1.2 - 1.3

. New icon - make sure you re-install the app to the system screen to see it.
. ROM Version added to menu
. New Mains adaptor - watch it change when you plug in the mains.
. Correctly identify Write protected, and all other types of SSD devices.
. New graphic pops up when battery level goes down to very low. Watch carefully.
. Reading of 1800 mV forces Main battery indicator to show "Low".


3FAX BATTERIES

Battery Checker will now read your 3Fax Battery status. The 3Fax doesn't
give you a "Good/Low" reading. It only shows you amount of time you have on
the batteries, and the date they were inserted. Choose this option from the
menu. NOTE: The 3Fax does not need to be connected for Battery Checker to give
you the readings. This is kept in an environment variable on your S3a.


CHECKING BATTERIES

One of the nicer functions of the Battery Checker is to check your battery
strength by putting a load on the battery, and watching it to see if it holds
up. This is the same way a battery in a car is checked. However, in order to
put a load on your S3a battery, the best way is to copy files over a 3Link.
Set some files to copy, and jump back into the Battery Checker. If you see
the main batteries jump down into the "Very Low" area, these batteries are
probably no good. If it jumps down into "Low", you may have a few hours left
on your batteries, unless they are nicads, in which case they may die any
minute. Of course, you have to experiment with your own batteries.

Battery Checker 1.3 will now show the condition of RAM SSD batteries, if and
only if they are the newer style RAM SSD's. Older RAM SSD's don't have the
necessary hardware to report battery levels. By default, Battery Checker shows
you both SSD Slots. You will see the SSD type on the bottom of the icon for
each SSD, labeled "A" and "B".  Types being "FLASH" , or "RAM".
