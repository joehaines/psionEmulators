BILLMATE TEXT    

THE BILLMATE ( 3a ) AND BILLMAT3  ( 3 ) ARE PROGRAMS WHICH I USE TO HELP 
ME CALCULATE BILLS BASED ON AN HOURLY RATE PLUS PARTS ( PARTS WHICH MAY OR
 MAY NOT INCLUDE VAT)     

THE PROGRAMS ARE SUPPLIED AS OPL FILES TO ALLOW ALTERATION OF THE HOURLY 
RATE AND THE VAT RATE .  THE APPROPRIATE PROGRAM FOR YOUR MACHINE SHOULD 
BE COPIED TO AN OPL DIRECTORY AND THEN EDITED TO ALLOW FOR YOUR HOURLY 
RATE ( DON'T FORGET TO ALTER THE DIALOG MIN AND MAX VALUES ALSO ) AND THEN 
TRANSLATED AND INSTALLED AS USUAL.  

PRESSING ESC AT ANY TIME CLOSES AND EXITS THE PROGRAM.
