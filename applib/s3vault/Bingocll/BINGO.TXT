BINGO Version 1.0
=================

BINGO is a Freeware Bingo Caller application for the PSION3a. 

Very handy if the family wish to play Bingo but either nobody 
wishes to be the caller or some of the numbers have been lost !


The following Files must be in the \WVE\ Directory on M:, A: or B:.

0.WVW
1.WVE
2.WVE
3.WVE
4.WVE
5.WVE
6.WVE
7.WVE
8.WVE
9.WVE

You may record these sounds using the built in RECORD application 
if you wish.

Enjoy !
