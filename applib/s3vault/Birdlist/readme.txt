Birdlist.spr

A Psion Spreadsheet listing the commoner birds visible from the UK. Just the thing for the Psion-owning Twitcher (OK, probably a fairly rare breed).

Installation instructions? I'm sure you don't need them. Just copy the Birdlist.spr file to an spr directory on your Psion and off you go. Then reformat it, change it around or add to it as you want.

Enjoy!
