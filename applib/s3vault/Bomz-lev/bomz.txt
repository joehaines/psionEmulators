ADDITIONAL LEVELS FOR BOMZ3A

These ten levels are harder than most of those included
with the original game, and are intended for players
who have completed all 80 (plus, perhaps, the bonus level).

Simply copy the ten files (suffixed .BOM) into a directory
on your 3a or 3c (preferably one named \BOMZ\ as this is
where the program will look first.

Choose LOAD from the Bomz menu (or press Psion-L), and
select CUSTOM00 from the dialog.

Note that, when you have completed custom level 0,
level 1 will load automatically, and so on.
