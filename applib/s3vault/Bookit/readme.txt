Bookit is intended to give a full analysis of income and
expenditure in relation to individual jobs, suppliers, 
materials and bank accounts.  It also gives the
opportunity to keep all records of suppliers, clients 
and bank accounts in one place.  Please find below a list of
features:-

Recording of direct debits, standing orders, invoices,
payments, deposits.  

Databases containing lists of clients, suppliers, and job
details.

Parking recorded as an expenditure against a job
and setting of alarm directly so parking tickets are a
thing of the past. 

Communications with pc or Macintosh to enable backing up
of all above files, at the simple press of a button.  

Printing of queries direct to attached printer 
(registered version only).

The removal of records at intervals in order to free up
memory on the psion, with the ability to re-import these
records at a later date and operating queries on them.
(Registered version only).


The Transaction List can be queried in such a way to display
information by job, account, or material/service type
i.e. if a builder was running a job at 24 Crescent Gardens
and wished to know his expenditure on this particular job,
of paint, this can be achieved by a simple query.

Installing Bookit
The above files should be copied to the directories below,

and Bookit should appear on the system screen.

Bookit.dbf should automatically appear as a database item and
can be used for help at any time.   Also once a knowledge of
