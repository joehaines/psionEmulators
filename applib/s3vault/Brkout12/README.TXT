Thanks you for download Shareware breakout game for PSION Series 3a.

INSTALLATION GUIDE.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

        UNZIP the Breakout.zip with -D parameter.

        and copy all files into PSION.

        Breakout.OPA in PSION "\APP" directory
        Other files  in PSION "\APP\BREAKOUT" directory and same drive 
                        of the program file.
                        (exclude this file)

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
For the unregistered user, you can only play up to level 2.
For the registered user, you can play all level (now, up to 20).
    and a boss level.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

Game feature (only for registered users) :-

        Password for entrance into individual level.
        Sound on and off feature.
        
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
How to play :-

        Use left/right arrow key to move your shield.

        You can collect the bonus bar, and got individual effects.
           
              Bonus Bar  Description                  Appear in which level
              ---------  ---------------------------  ---------------------
                 _____
                (__E__)  Enlarge your shield.         (Level 1 or up)
                 _____
                (__S__)  Shorten your shield.         (Level 4 or up)
                 _____
                (__X__)  Extra enlarge your shield.   (Level 3 or up)
                 _____
                (__P__)  Bottom protect line.         (Level 2 or up)
                 _____
                (__«__)  Double speed your ball.      (Level 5 or up)

        Use Esc Key to pause the game.

        Use Menu key to got other features.

        Use Help key to got user guide.

        Every 1500 Score, will got a bonus shield.
