Car
by Graham Mulholland
Wizards Ltd - 0494 837515

Car is a expense tracking program for cars and other vehicles. It allows the recording of journeys (including whether business or personal) and other expenditure (petrol, servicing, etc.). A summary, between selected dates, may be produced on screen or printed out. Functions such as file import/export, auditing and help are catered for as external modules, available separately from the main program.
