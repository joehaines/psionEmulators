Description/Overview:
	Automobile Lease was written to help determine monthly lease
	prices on new automobiles.  The formula was attained when I was
	leasing my last automobile and should cover 90% of all leases.  
	
	And as you will see, sometimes it doesn't pay to lease a "cheaper"
	automobile.  The residual value of the higher end manufacturers, 
	BMW, Mercedes, Rover, etc is significantly higher at the end of the 
	lease.  The higher residual value can sometimes make the payment of 
	a BMW equal to that of a lesser priced Ford or GM.

	Residual value is determined by the manufacturer and is based upon
	the length of the lease term (how much percentage is the auto worth
	after a period of time),,, most vehicles are less than 50% after
	three years.
	
	Rate Factor is a lease variable set by the manufacturer depending 
	upon specials or incentives they may have be offering
	
	State taxes change from state to state, please verify with your
	dealer for specifics.  

	State usage taxes are also state specific and are very uncommon, 
	please verify with your dealer if your home state requires usage
	taxes.

Install Automobile Lease:
	- Copy "carlease.opa" to the \APP\ directory on any drive on the 3a
	- Install "carlease.opa" using Psion-I from the System screen.
	- carlease will create CARLEASE.INI in M:\OPD

Some Help:
	- Percentage rates are entered in as normally stated. 
	  (Enter 8.5 for 8.5%  NOT  .085)
