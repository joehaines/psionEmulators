Running a small mail order business, it's not been possible for us to
afford the expensive credit card swipe machine that connects direct
to the bank to post the transaction. We have always mailed in vouchers,
but we are consequently at risk from fraud. To reduce the risk, we
check the card numbers and make sure they pass the standard checksum
tests. We've used our own Windows software to do this for many years. We
recently decided that actually, the best way to work would be to check
the validity while the customer was on the phone, but the PC is never in
the right state to start the check program. So we ported it to the Psion.

The enclosed software works well enough for us to trust our own business
to it, but obviously your needs differ. If you find CredChek is useful,
something you'd like to keep on your Psion, please register. The software
is fully functional and all registering will do (apart from us sending
a certificate with a code number to remove the "unregistered" word) 
is encourage us to maintain it. We're sure you won't abuse our trust.
If you find something it doesn't do, or even worse something it does
wrong, please tell us and we'll try to fix it. We are keen to know
the checksum algorithms for the cards we don't take, such as JCB.
If the program saves you from loss, please send us the anecdote
(preferably clipped to a percentage of the saving!)
