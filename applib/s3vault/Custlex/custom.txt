Customer Lexicon for PSION Siena
----------------------------

Introduction
------------
The long awaited conversion from the PSION Series 3a/3c is now
well and truly over. Customer Lexicon for the Siena is now here,
large as life and just as addictive.

a full database utility for customer based information, address and
account info, mailing labels, full password protectio etc.


Installation
------------
Copy CUSTOM.opa onto the \APP directory of your Siena
Copy REGS.lex onto the \OPD directory of your Siena

Select INSTALL and the select CUSTOM.OPA and youre ready to go
