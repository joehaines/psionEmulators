This is a utility to convert Agenda files from the Series 3a format to
the Series 3 format. It is translated to be S3 compatible so runs on
both machines.

As the S3a format is far more complex than the S3 there have had to be
many compromises. If there are more than 50 repeating items then this
is flagged and further repeating items are made non-repeating and
shown on the first day the repeat should happen. All To-Do lists are
amalgamated into one. Anniversaries are combined with un-timed repeats
and made un-timed entries.

As with all my software, I suspect there are bugs - I have only been
able to check the program with my own Agenda files. If you get
problems then, unless you can give me sufficient information to
replicate the problem on my machine, the best chance I will have of
sorting your problem will be with a copy of your offending Agenda
file. This brings into question security and this is a question only
you can answer - do you let me have a copy of your file and risk
security, or do you live with the problem? It's up to you - obviously,
I will maintain security on your file to the best of my ability, but I
