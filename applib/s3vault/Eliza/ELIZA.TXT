                             Eliza
                             -----
This is a simple psion 3 version of the classic Eliza. Eliza is a computer psychiatrist, who is there to help you with your problems! For best results in the session, try to use a variety of methods to express yourself and talk about your problems. eg. I don't feel to good, I have a problem, I am not feeling to good, etc.

To install the game you must copy the necessary files into your APP directory. There are three files - Eliza3.app, Eliza3a.app and Eliza.pic. There are two versions of Eliza. Eliza3.app is for the Psion series 3 and Eliza3a.app is for the Psion series 3a. Copy the relevant app into your APP directory, and then copy the Eliza.pic file into your APP directory. If you now select install from the Apps menu, Eliza will be installed on the icon bar. 

Note: Both versions may be played on the Series 3a. The Series 3 version may be preferred as it has a bigger font.
