GAMBLE CRAZY V1.01 - By Mike McCarthy
------------------

WHAT IS GAMBLE CRAZY?

Gamble crazy is a slot machine for the Psion 3a/3c I wrote one art lesson
to pass the time. Some of my friends saw it when I finished. They themselves
loved gambling machines and were addicted after their first go.

HOW DO I PLAY?

Press SPACE to spin the reels. If you get a gamble, press 'G' to Gamble the
money you have won for double the amount or 'C' to Collect it without risk.
If you get a HOLD, press 1,2 or 3 to hold a reel and press 'D' when you are
finished, choose carefully as there is no cancel feature on the holds.
When you run out of credits and don't have enough money to pay for the next
level you lose.

HOW DO I INSTALL THIS?

If you used PKUNZIP make sure you use the -d option. I think Unzip.IMG
restores the directory structure. Once unzipped copy all the files onto your
Internal Disk. This does not work from RAM card yet so don't try it. If you
don't get the directory structure back it should be:-
GAMBLE.OPA       in     \APP\
All PIC files    in     \GAMES\GAMBLE\
