INTRODUCTION

Most of the competitions available should be familiar to regular golfers but some of these games need an explanation on their use.


INDIVIDUAL COMPETITIONS

All the competitions excluding Alliance competitions, Skins and Matchplay are individual competitions.  The scores and results for the individuals are calculated and displayed in the main part of the screen.  In addition to individual scores the betterball results are displayed near the top-right of the screen alongside the "A+B" and "C+D" labels.  These competitions therefor double as betterball competitions.


ALLIANCE COMPETITIONS

Alliance competitions calculate the best score of all four scores and is displayed alongside the "C+D" label.  Individual scores are also calculated and displayed in the normal manner.


SKINS

Skins can be played with 2, 3 or 4 players.  The player who wins the hole out-right is marked and the number of holes won is displayed as the result.  Holes not won out-right are carried over to the next hole.  An out-right win is defined as the player with the best nett score with NO other player equaling this score.


MATCHPLAY

Matchplay is played with two players only.  Any of the four players can play a matchplay competition and so must be indicated by marking the scores of the first hole of the two players involved in the matchplay competition.
