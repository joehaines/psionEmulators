HANGMAN aka WORD-FIND

Copy the OPL files to your OPL directory and the two DBF files to the DAT
directory.

Two databases are provided:
HANGMAN1.DBF   (200 English words)
HANGMAN2.DBF   (500 German words)

Use to database application or the program GEHANG.OPL to change the databases.
Feel free to add your own words but please share the databases with us!
