Psychrometric Calculation Suite
===============================

I wrote this little program some years ago for the now ancient
LZ64 Psion II (remember those things?). I've now re-coded it
for the Series 3 (a&c).

For anyone who does not know, psychrometrics are the study
of the properties of humid air - Something many of us may already
have unwittingly participated in after a good night of beer
and vindaloo.

Unless you are in the air conditioning, or HVAC fields of
engineering, this program will soon loose it's novelty
value.

The program is pretty self explanatory.

Accuracy of results is very close to the CIBSE published charts
up to temperatures of around 60c and then it becomes gradually
more and more inacurate.


File list

HVAC.OPO	The Psion series 3 program. Put in \OPO and run it!
HVAC.TXT	This file with my current email address.
HVAC.EXE	MS Windows program, for your further edification.
HVAC.DOC	MS Windows program documentation.

I have also included HVAC.exe, a MS windows program (needs
vbrun300.dll) that offers the same calculations, plus dew point
in a more user friendly way, with a print facility.
