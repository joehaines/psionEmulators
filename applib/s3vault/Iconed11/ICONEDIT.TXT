ICONEDIT Ver 1.0

Icon editor for the Psion Series 3a.

The first thing I had to do when I got my Series 3a was get rid of some of
those UGLY Series 3 icons. So here's my first bit of 3a OPL work, and a couple
of example icons.

Installation:
Copy ICONEDIT.OPA to your APP directory.
Copy the demo PIC files to your OPD directory (if you want them).
Install the ICONEDIT app on your system screen.


Hope you like it.
