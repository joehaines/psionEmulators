Icon and Painter Program
========================

Freeware!

A useful painting program for the Psion 3a

Simple creation of desktop icons using a 48 x 48 grab box.

Draw shapes and objects in white, grey or black.

Save or load any size bitmap.

Paste in original colour or alternative.

On line help screen.

Hope somebody finds it useful!!
