
ICONEDIT - simple Icon editor for the Psion Series 3(A).

ICONEDIT edits S3 icons (which can also be used on the 3A). Note that it does
not support the S3A icons which are larger and make use of the S3 grey plane.
Iconedit uses two cursors: the `point' and the `mark' - this will be a familiar
concept to users of the Emacs text editors. All point editting occurs at the
`point', i.e. setting and clearing points. All other operations use both the
`point' and `mark', e.g. line between `point' and `mark', cut-paste operations
in the rectangle defined by the `point' and `mark'. All operations are
available off the menu and with keyboard shutcuts - note that you do not have
to hold down the Psion key to use the shortcuts. The rest should be fairly
self-explanatory from the menus.

ICONEDIT is `postcard-ware'. You are free to use it and give copies away to
others - but send me some feedback on a postcard. I do not expect any money and
there is no registration service. However, if you use it a lot a small donation
would encourage me to provide support and add any features you may like. I am
considering making it an application (OPA) with on-line help, and providing
different modes for the paste operations (merge/replace/invert). If there is
sufficient interest I may produce a 3A version - I don't own a 3A myself but
have access for testing, so you will have to persuade me that it is worth
implementing!
