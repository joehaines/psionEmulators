WHAT DOES IT DO?

The Jock Clock allows broadcasters, technical operators, studio managers,
disc-jockeys and hospital radio presenters to work out timings of discs / 
tapes / commercials in order to hit the news jingle, pips, or other programme 
junction on time.  

NB:  This SHAREWARE version of The Jock Clock, calculates to a news jingle 
     that is 13 seconds long.  When you register the programme, you simply
     state the duration of your own news jingle which will be incorporated
     into your own copy of the programme.  Also, the "calculate to junction" 
     function is only available in the registered version.



COMPATABILITY:

The Jock Clock has been written specifically for the Psion S3A and WILL NOT
WORK on the older Psion 2 or Psion 3 models.  However, The Jock Clock will
work on the excellent PC Emulator (S3AEMUL) available from all good bulletin
boards!

INSTALLATION:

Presumably, you have already un-zipped the two files README.TXT (this one) 
and JOCK.OPA, so all you need to do is copy JOCK.OPA to the \APP directory
on your Psion S3A.  Then install JOCK.OPA as an application in the normal way  

If installing onto the PC emulator, copy JOCK.OPA into an \APP sub-directory 
of your main emulator files.

OPERATION:

Dialog boxes and nag screens are cleared by pressing the ENTER or RETURN key.

Press the MENU key for the various programme options, which will give you the
hot-keys to use, once you are more familiar with the programme.
