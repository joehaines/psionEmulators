1) Office.IBL and Office.IBN comprise a small add on image library for
   use with IconBox v2.01.  IconBox is the Psion Series 3a Icon library
   and generator from Right Hand Software Ltd.

2) The images in Office.IBL are those of the main Microsoft Apps - W4W,
   Excel, Powerpoint, Access, Project and VB.  I'm sure you can find
   more sensible uses for them (maybe) but it might be amusing to use
   them in conjunction with aliases of the S3a apps (lists) eg: W4W icon
   + Word, Excel+Sheet, VB+Program etc and see how many people you can
   fool into thinking you've got Windows on your S3a - probably not many
   and would you want it anyway? ;-)
