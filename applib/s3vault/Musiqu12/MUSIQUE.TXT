Musique v1.2  By John Portwin
============

The Psion 3a Music Editor and Player
====================================

What is it?
-----------

Musique is a fully featured, multi channel music player and editor for the
Psion 3a. If you don't know what a Psion 3a is, you've just wasted a
download ;-)

Musique is better than its competitors because you can make your Psion play
*two* channels of music AT THE SAME TIME! The note editor doesn't rely on
overcomplicated staves and musical notation - it simply says the name, octave
and duration of the note in plain English. So even if you can't read music,
you can still use Musique!

Once you've entered your notes you can change the tempo of the overall song,
transpose parts of it, even give it a name and title. Then you can save it
in a very compact format - just 4 bytes per note!

What do I do with it?
---------------------

All you need to do is copy MUSIQUE.OPA into your \APP directory on your Psion.
That's it! No messy data files, picture files, etc. Then install it (from the
system screen) by pressing PSION-I together.

Then highlight the Musique icon (with "Musique" displayed below it) and press
enter. This will load Musique, and you will see the title screen. A directory
\MSF will automatically be created on your disk. Then you will be shown the
new song dialog box.

How do I do it?
---------------

Now enter the name you want for the song and the composer's name. You can
then specify how many channels you want your song to have.

Press enter and select the filename you want to save as.

Then you are taken to the main menu. Under the "Song" menu, select Editor,
and then you are taken to the note editor. I and A insert and add notes
(select the frequency, octave and duration) - delete erases the currently
highlighted note and the arrow keys move the highlight.

There are more keys - press help to find out about them.

Now to play your newly entered song, quit back to the main menu by hitting
escape. Move down the Song menu to Play (making sure Sound is set to on in
the system screen!) and your new song will be played.

The other main menu options are pretty self-explanatory - you can review
the online help which explains them all.

Musique works just like any normal application - Psion-N over the system
screen's icon lets you enter the filename for a new file, while enter opens
the currently highlighted file.

Limitations
-----------

One of my personal dislikes is software that is so limited you can hardly
do anything with it. Musique is not like that. All the key menu options
are available in the unregistered version - load, save, the editor of course,
and the player as well. The only real limitation is that you are limited to
a maximum of two hundred notes on each channel.

But if you register, you will get the following benefits:

* A maximum of 1024 notes per channel (enough for anyone's needs, but if you
  do require more, just let me know - the only limit is memory)
* You can merge two music files together
* You can play specific parts of files instead of the whole thing (comes in
  very useful for logner tunes!)
* You can transpose a range of notes on either channel
* And the clear conscience that is assosiated with registering quality 
  software!
