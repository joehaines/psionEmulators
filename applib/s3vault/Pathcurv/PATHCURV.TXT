These two little programs draw 'Path Curve' forms on your Psion.  Load
them into the opl directory and run them.  They are self explanetory
and actually very similar.  Pathcurv draws single curves. Pathcrv2 draws
multiple curves.  Adjust the numbers in the second dialogue to change
the forms.

I include my humble code incase you wish to play with it.
