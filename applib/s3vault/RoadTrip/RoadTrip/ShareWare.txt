ShareWare Information
---------------------

"RoadTrip" is ShareWare and thus has two items crippled:

 * A maximum of 10 links per trip.
 * No saving of trips to disk.

These restrictions might sound harsh, but they're not: You can still
fully and completely test the navigation side of the program to your
heart's content.  (If I allowed saving, you could simply create an
entire journey made up of 10-link blocks and load them in as needed).
Once you've tested the program by experimenting with short 10-link
trips, you are ready to register for the full program, which lets you
save your trips and gives you 999 links per trip.

The registration fee is charged by the method that you want to receive
the full program.  These are (shown in Australian dollars):

 * E-Mail (zipped archive): $10
 * MS-DOS floppy diskette : $20

The floppy diskette fee is higher due to the postage charges involved,
especially when shipping overseas.  I can only ship on MS-DOS disks,
so users of other systems (Amiga, Macintosh, etc) will have to rely on
their own skills to get around this inconvenience.  Sorry!  :(

All prices are in Australian dollars and you must convert your dollar
value to meet this.  I can only accept cash or an International Money
Order from overseas purchasers, but Australian residents can E-Mail me
to get my bank account number to do a Direct Deposit.

At the time of this writing I do not own my own home, so I don't want
to give a postal address for orders.  Please contact me via E-Mail to
obtain details on where to send your payment: otser@yahoo.com

Thank you!
