Updating RoadTrip
-----------------

This release of "RoadTrip" (v1.1) is relatively new and thus may have
bugs or other navigational problems of which I am currently unaware.
Also, driving conditions around the world may be vastly different to
what you find in the program, and thus may lead you to wishing that it
did a certain thing or had links for a certain situation.

In these cases, please E-Mail me!  :)  I am not a very travelled man
and I would gladly update RoadTrip to accommodate international road
conditions.  Don't just sit there wishing it did something: Make it
happen by E-Mailing me at otser@yahoo.com and I'll see what I can do!
