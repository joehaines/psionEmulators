SNABANK Personal Accounts Suite for the Siena
---------------------------------------------

For those familiar with S3ABANK, the only major changes you will see are to take account of the limited screen space.For example, there is no option to select the middle column display, becasuse there isn't a middle column.  Similarly, the credit and debit columns are always combined. However, in most of the screens there is an additional menu item which gives all the details for an entry.  Use <PSION-I> or just <I> to access it.

Note the files have changed names, so refer to the README.TXT file for installation instructions.
