TITLE: SOLITAIR.LZH  (4550 Bytes)
  Solitaire, a solo player card game.  Now available on a Series 3, near you!


  Solitair.lzh contains SOLITAIR.OPA (6528 bytes), which goes into the APP 
folder, and CARDS.FON (2432 bytes), which goes into the OPD folder of the 
same device.


  Instructions are available from the menu, but basically, you have to sort
the cards into four columns by suit and in decending order, from the King
down to the Ace.  A face-up card, along with all cards below it in the column,-

can be moved only to a free card that is both the same suit and one higher in -

rank.



  Please enjoy.  Dave.
