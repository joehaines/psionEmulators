Here is a new collection of UK Dialing STD code databases for the
3a/3a/(Sienna?), which updates a little bit the STDLON.DBF database
that is in some Psion Archives.

There are 3 variants, consisting of 3 parts of the database:

STD     4465 entry database of UK National Dialing codes
LON	     764 entry database of London (Inner & Outer) Dialing codes
Xcodes		  20 entry database of Extra codes, UK Mobile Phone networks
and premium rate phoneline STDs etc.

They are very usefull for finding dialing codes and for reverse
lookups of codes from missed calls that leave caller ID info, you can
find out where the call came from geographically.

The 3 sections are arranged as 3 seperate .ZIP files:

stdlonx.zip   STD & LON codes + extra codes - 5250 entries, 148kb
londonx.zip   LON codes + extra codes - 784 entries, 40kb
stdx.zip      STD & extra codes only  - 4486 entries, 110kb
              (for people not interested in London codes)


These 3 databases compare as follows to the other Psion databases
floating around the archives as follows (from FrontierNet):


std.zip              Database file of STD codes 
                     (Much smaller, no London or Mobiles)

stdlon.zip           Another Database of STD codes
                     (I think I might have uploaded this one, but the
new
                      ones replace it).
