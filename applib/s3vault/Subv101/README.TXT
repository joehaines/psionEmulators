STORY
~~~~~

Hidden in the ocean's depths are many wonders. It is your job to collect
samples of the wealth of different crystals which form in the underwater 
caverns. You have control the NRJ-25 sub which was specially developed 
for this type of environment. Due to the extreme danger of diving at these 
depths your air supply is supported at the cave's opening, with a connecting 
pipe your only life support the mission is full of danger. DO YOU ACCEPT???

INSTALLING
~~~~~~~~~~

Copy the file SUBMIS.OPA into your \APP\ directory.
Copy the remaining files into the directory \APP\SUBMIS\

NOTE : The SUBDAT.1 to SUBDAT.4 files are needed. The SUBSND files are
not but the game may sound strange unless you turn the sound off.

HOW TO PLAY
~~~~~~~~~~~

The air pipe connected to the sub is your lifeline. You cannot double back 
on yourself, instead you must retract the air cable to move back. Many 
dangerous creatures inhabit the caves. If they find your air supply they 
will bite through the cable causing the sub to implode!
The sub is covered with a force field which can be used to stun the 
creatures, but only the sub is protected. 

KEYS
~~~~
Version 1.00 allowed the use of Cursor Keys. This has been removed.
Believe me these controls are easier to use and the control code has
been revamped to make them even better!! These keys are much more
natural when "holding" the Psion.

UP      - A
DOWN    - Z
LEFT    - ,
RIGHT   - .
RETRACT - SHIFT
PAUSE   - ENTER

QUIT	- Escape
