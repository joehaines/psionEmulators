
		Temperature Error Correction (TEC) Spreadsheet
		**********************************************


1.	This is small spreadsheet for the psion-3a which will work out TEC.

2.	For each approach it will require the accurate:

	Threshold elevation (feet)
	Airfield pressure alt (feet)
	Surface temp (deg C)

	There is space for upto 8 check heights on each approach, these are
	entered in the "Req HAT" (Required Height Above Threshold) column. 
	The column immediately to the right gives the TEC. The next 2 give
	the Indicated QFE height and the Indicated QNH altitude.

3.	The corrections given will be good for airfields upto 1000 feet 
	pressure alt and check heights upto 6000 feet HAT  (assuming press 
	alt,surf temp,etc are entered accurately.)

	of use of this program.  However, no liability can be assumed for
