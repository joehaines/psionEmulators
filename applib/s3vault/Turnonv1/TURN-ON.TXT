Turn-on v1.0 by C.K.Holland 1998.

Description:

A small utility, (1k), that allows one to play
a sound file, (*.wve), upon turning on the Psion.
The sound played s chosen by the user.Memory useage 
is quite small, (about 10k), but may be more
if a large sound file is played...so have a 
good amount of memory free for large files.

Machines:

Series 3c, but you are
welcome to try it on 3a at your own
risk. Not suitable for the Series 5.

Installation:

Put Turn-on.opa into the APP directory on the
internal RAM drive. Install in usual way from
the system screen. Run and follow the instructions.
You must have at least one .wve file in the
Internal drive's WVE directory for the program to
work. If you don't then the program will not
play a sound.
