WebSurf Interface

WebSurf has got an interface that has been kept as near as possible to the other Series 3a applications, i.e. menus, dialog boxes, zooming, switchable status window and multiple languages are supported.
You can try now to zoom in or out with the menu commands. Press Menu and choose "Zoom in" or "Zoom out".

WebSurf knows many HTML elements, and the list will grow in future versions.
