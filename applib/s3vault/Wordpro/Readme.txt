WORDPRO is an educational macro that tracks vocabulary words. Whether
you are reading an e-book, writing that bestseller, or keeping up with
academic doublespeak, WordPro can help you build a powerful vocabulary. 
It requires Tom Doblin's Macro System and the Spell/Thesaurus program.

WORDPRO has two dictionaries (large and small), commonly misspelled words (sic),
a list of feelings (beats "fine"), tracks your own wordlist, a full rhyming
dictionary, and lots of self-help tips. Registered users may request a Word 
link for easy spell checking of their documents, receive all new updates,
and get all of the other Protronics apps.

INSTALLATION

File        Directory   Description
----------- ----------- -----------------------------------------------------
*.TXT       \TXT        Words to Supercharge Your Vocabulary (optional)
EDITOR.ALS  \APP        Use to edit all *.TXT files (Psion-I installs)
RHYMES.DBF  \DAT        A Complete Rhyming Dictionary of the English Language
WORDPRO.MCO \MACRO\MCO\ A macro for tracking and reviewing new words

Assign WORDPRO.MCO to Ctrl-W in Macro. The beta version may require
that you edit WORDPRO.MCR if you have Spell on a SSD card rather than
built into ROM. Once installed, highlight a word and press Ctrl-W.
You can press the first letter of a word to jump to it in the lookup
list. If you are unable to highlight a word or you want to type one in,
select New and enter your word. If nothing on your Psion is highlighted,
you may get an alert from the Macro System. Just press Enter to continue.
When editing be sure to save *.TXT files as Text, not a Word format.
