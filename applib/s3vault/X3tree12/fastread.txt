Installing X3Tree
.................

X3Tree.opa    >   \APP\
X3Tree2.opo   >   \APP\X3Tree\
help.opo      >   \APP\X3Tree\  (optional)
diskview.opo  >   \APP\X3Tree\  (optional)

Install the files in the directories shown above. If you are desperately
short of space then you can leave out help.opo and diskview.opo.
Diskview.opo is a graphical add on program that will appear in the menu when
installed.
