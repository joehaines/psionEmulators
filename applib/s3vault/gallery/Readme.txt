USING GALLERY (PSION S3A).

You need to put these 13 files in their respective directories:
-------------------------------------
in "\PIC\" you need
Author1.pic
Author2.pic
Border1.pic
Border2.pic
Border3.pic
Border4.pic
Border5.pic
Border6.pic
Iconpic.pic

in "\OPD\" you need
Howto.hlp
About.hlp
Pictures.dat

in "\APP\" you need
Gallery.opa
-------------------------------------

Installation:
-------------
1. unzip gallery.zip
2. put each of the 13 files in the directories, as above.
3. Go to the psion's SYSTEM screen.
4. Press psion-I (Install). Choose Name: Gallery.opa, Disk: Internal, Position: Last. Press Enter.
5. press psion-A (Assign button to 'gallery'). Use left & right arrow keys until 'Control+Time'
is shown. Press Enter now.


Using the program:
------------------
 Use "add a new picture" from MENU to add pictures to your gallery. These pictures must be
".PIC" files stored in the "\PIC\" directory. Once you have selected a picture, choose from
6 picture frames. Position the picture as you wish. 
 Press TAB to advance through the pictures in your gallery.
 I've included 2 pics (of ME heh heh!) to start you off. Press TAB & delete if necessary.
 Save your gallery layout at any time. If you leave the program running, just press
CONTROL+TIME to view your gallery.

Hints:
------
 This is how I got the 2 pictures of the author into the PSION.
1. I scanned b&w photo at 100dpi at size=50%
2. used Paintshop Pro (by JASC) to alter the size & crop.
3. saved the image twice. Once as a .pcx version 5, allowing 256 greyscale,
and then as a .pcx version 2, allowing 16 greyscale. (Just select from "Save As" menu option)
4. Copied the 2 .pcx files to the \PIC\ directory of my PSION
5. Used 2 excellent programs, Firepic (Firecrest software) & Draw2.8 from Choclate Island Software to Import the .pcx files & saved them as .pic files. *Experiment with settings.
I got these two programs from the Psion Shareware Library, run by Steve Litchfield.
