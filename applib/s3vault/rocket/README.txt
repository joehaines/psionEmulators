            
            README for Rocket V1.0
            
            What is Rocket?
            
            Rocket is a game for the Psion Siena that pays homage to THRUST for the Acorn BBC.
            
            The idea is to navigate your way to the small black platform, without hitting anything. You will then progress onto the next level.
            
            Keys
            
            P for power, to cancel out the force of gravity, and go upwards.
            
            X for left thrust
            
            C for right thrust
            
            Preferences
            
            Sound - Turns the sound on or off, as palmtop sound can be irritating to others.
            
            Gravity - You can alter the gravitational pull of the planet, I like it on normal.
            
            Borders - Do you want borders on your obstacles? Purely a cosmetic feature.
            
            Notes
            
            Out of memory errors can occur when playing Rocket for a long time without restarting  Rocket.
