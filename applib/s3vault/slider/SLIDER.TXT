SLIDER

A Psion version of those plastic sliding square 
puzzles.  Very playable, and under 4k in size.


INSTALLATION

Simply place the file SLIDER.OPA into any APP 
directory, then install from the System screen in 
the usual way. 


PLAYING

First select the pattern to aim for - there are 12 to 
choose from, or you can let the Psion select at 
random.  This pattern is shown on the right of the 
screen.   After the tiles on the left have been 
shuffled, use the arrow keys to change the pattern  on the left to match that on the right.  

DELETE undoes the last move.

Sound options: ALL gives a sound every time a tile 
moves, SOME when the shuffle is complete and 
when you finish the puzzle.
