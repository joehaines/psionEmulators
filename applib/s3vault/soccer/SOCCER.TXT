                         SOCCER 3A README
                       ====================

Installation
------------
Put the soccer.opa in the app directory.
Make a directory called SOCCER and put the rest of the files in it.
"Install" from the menu and select "soccer.opa".Immediately the soccer 3a
icon will appear on the system screen.Press enter on top of it and 
enjoy!

Instructions
------------
-When the player appears,press menu key.
-Select New game.
-Select your team and number of players(1 or 2).
-Select a team for the CPU or the second player.
-When the shoot screen appears,select a number from 1 to 5 to shoot.
