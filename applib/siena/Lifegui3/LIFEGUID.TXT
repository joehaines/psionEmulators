LIFEGUIDE is a bible-introduction for the Psion Series 3a/3c and Siena.
========================================================================

To install LIFEGUIDE, place LIFEGUID.OPA into the \APP subdirectory on
any Psion disk drive and install the application in the usual way, with
Psion-I.
