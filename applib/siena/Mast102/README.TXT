(At last, another freeware game!)

A mastermind-like game by L.M. de Vries (100121.3102@compuserve.com)
(Homepage: http://ourworld.compuserve.com/homepages/platodva/psion.htm)

How to play the game:

1. Use setup from the menu to set the number of letters you want to use.
2. Start a new game.
3. You have to guess(?) the combination the s3a/Siena has made for you.
4. Use the letter-keys and the up/down arrow-keys to do the input.
5. After you entered 4 letters, press enter, and the program will tell you
   how you did. A black block means the right letter at the right place. A
   white block means that the letter is in the combination but at the wrong
   place.
6. Guess/deduce the combination it within 10 minutes.

Other otions:
1. Give up: If you can't figure it out, use this menu-option.


If you have any suggestions (bugs!?!?), please mail them to me.

Have fun.
