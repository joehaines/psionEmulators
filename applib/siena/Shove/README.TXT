Shove 1.00

Here's a little game for you. For free. If you like simple and small games
you got the right one here. 
It just takes a little over 2 Kb on your disk and about 5 Kb to run.

The goal is to get all the 15 numbered blocks in place. 
Starting with the '1' in the top left corner. 
Use the 4 cursor keys to achive this.
It gives you a beep when ready (so you know when to stop)
