FUEL CONSUMPTION MONITOR (SIENA Only)
-------------------------------------

History:

29 Sep 96	Initial Issue of V1.0 based on V1.5 of S3AFUEL for the S3A/S3C

26 Nov 96	Version 1.1 issued with the following enhancements:

	a.	Swedish Date format added (YY-MM-DD)
	b.	Ltrs/10kms added as consumption option.
	c.	A new 'Type' field added to denote normal/urban/long-distance driving.
	d.	Edit Memo now has a separate memo for each car.

15 Jan 97	Version 1.2 issued with the following enhancements:

	a.	Statistics screen has been enhanced.
	b.	A new Oil Change/Servicing reminder has been implemented.
