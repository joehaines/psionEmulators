Introduction
------------

Subway is a Psion 3a program for finding your way around the London Underground.


Installation
------------

The following files need to be copied to the Psion.

Subway.opa          \APP
Sub$way.img         \APP\SUBWAY
London.map          \APP\SUBWAY

Subway can then be installed onto the system screen.
