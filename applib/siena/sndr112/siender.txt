Overview

Siender is Defender for your Psion Siena - a fast and addictive arcade
game.  This is shareware - please feel free to try it out and see what
you think, then register if you like it.

Installation

To install:
	- copy App\siender.app to directory \App\ on your Psion
	- Install Siender using `Install' below the Apps menu on the system
	  screen in the usual way.
