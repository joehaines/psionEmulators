Alarm activation.



When the Alarm is activated, the Alarm! window will appear in order to tell you that an Alarm time has been reached for the date and time which you specified earlier.

You can now:

  1. Set Alarm to snooze for a period in hours and minutes before sounding again.
  2. Mute the Alarm by tapping the Mute button in the Alarm! window.
  3. Cancel the Alarm by pressing the Dismiss button in the Alarm! window.

Preferences.



Select the Format of the clock display from Preferences.

Formats include:

  • Analogue format: to create an analogue clock on the Psion LX button bar.
  • 12 hour format: to create a digital clock display on the Psion LX button bar in 12 hour format.
  • 24 hour format: to create a digital clock display on the Psion LX button bar in 24 hour format.
  • show seconds: to create a clock in the other three formats showing seconds.
