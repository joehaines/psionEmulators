Alarms.



To set an alarm for a Calendar entry:

  1. Move the cursor to the entry.
  2. Tap and hold the stylus against the screen until the Calendar: Edit event dialog box appears. Click on the Alarm tab, toggle the Alarm button on and set the alarm to go off at a pre-determined period before the event.


You can also set an alarm while adding an entry by tapping the Alarm button on the panel and selecting the Alarm option.

Entries which have alarms are marked with a bell symbol. To remove an alarm, tap and hold the stylus against the screen at the entry whose alarm you wish to deactivate. Select the Alarm tab from the Calendar: Edit event dialog box and toggle the Alarm button off to deactivate the alarm.

At the pre-determined time, the Alarm will sound and you can mute, dismiss or set the Alarm on snooze.
