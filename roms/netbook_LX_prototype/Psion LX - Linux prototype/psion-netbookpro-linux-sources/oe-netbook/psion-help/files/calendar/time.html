Timed & untimed events.



To enter a "timed" or "untimed" day entry:

Move the cursor to the date of the appointment or reminder. Tap and hold the stylus against the screen where you have entered your appointment or reminder. The Calendar Edit event dialog box will appear. If the appointment needs a start and end time, enter the information in the appropriate box. To set an untimed reminder, select Reminder from the drop down menu and click OK or for timed reminders, tap the Time toggle and enter the time in the appropriate box.

A timed appointment appears with the start and end time of the appointment beside the text in the Calendar window. A timed reminder appears with the time of the reminder beside the text in the Calendar window. Appointments and reminders are shown against a coloured background within the planner for the current day.

You can set the duration or end time of a timed entry in the New entry dialog.
