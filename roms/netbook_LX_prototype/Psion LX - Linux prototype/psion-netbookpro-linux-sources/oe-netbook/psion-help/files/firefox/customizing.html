Customizing the display



You can use the display settings to show more of a page on the screen.

To change the size of the information on the screen and to hide the Firefox menu bar and Psion LX panel:

  • Press F11 or go to or select Full Screen from the View menu or select Text size from the View menu.

To show or hide the Navigation and/or Bookmarks Toolbars:

  • choose Toolbars from the View menu and select or deselect the appropriate bar.
  • To show or hide the sidebar which displays either the history of the pages which you have visited or your Bookmarks:
  • tap on Sidebar which is available from the View Menu.

To change the fonts used in the browser window:

  • select Fonts and Colours from the General Preferences tab (go to Tools/Downloads/Preferences).
