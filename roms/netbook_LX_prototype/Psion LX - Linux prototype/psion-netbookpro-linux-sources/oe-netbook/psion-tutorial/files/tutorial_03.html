The Desktop: information, tasks, and minor programs

Press the Desk button in the bottom left of the screen to see the Desktop.

There is a selector down the side of the screen for you to choose between these three screens.

Today: this tells you information that you might want to know at the current point in time, such as the number of unread emails, your next appointment from the Calendar, and so on.
Programs: this is like the Desktop on a PC, as it just lists all the available programs.
Tasks: these are a selection of useful or everyday actions, described in plain English.

Alternatively, you can just press the Desk button repeatedly to move between them.
