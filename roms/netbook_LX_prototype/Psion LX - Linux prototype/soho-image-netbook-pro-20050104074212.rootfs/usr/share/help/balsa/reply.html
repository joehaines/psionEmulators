Replying to & forwarding emails



You can respond to an email whilst you are reading it.

To reply to or forward an email:

  • Tap the Reply button to reply only to the sender of the original email.
  • Tap the Reply to All button to reply to everyone who received the original email.
  • Tap Forward to forward a copy of the original email to someone else.

You can now type in your response in the "message editor". You can change the message address(es), subject, or add an attachment in the same way as you would when writing a new message.
