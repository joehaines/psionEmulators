Sending & recieving email.



When you're ready to connect and send your email, make sure all the messages you want to send are in the Out folder.

Note: Make sure that your modem is connected to your Psion LX.


To send and receive email:

  • Click on the Send/Check button on the menu bar.

Copies of emails that have been sent are placed in the Sent folder; whilst messages which couldn't be sent are left in the Out box. Copies of received emails are received in the In folder.

Note: Delete messages you no longer require from the Sent folder to save disk space.
