General calculations.



To perform a calculation in Basic mode:

  1. Enter your calculation using the number and operator buttons (+, -, * and � on screen or the keyboard. There is operator precedence in the Basic Mode: (10+5)*3=45 but 10+5*3=25 .
  2. Tap = or press Enter to get the result.
  • Use +/- to change the sign of a number.
  • To clear the calculation line without ending the current calculation, press C.
  • To clear the calculation line and end the current calculation, press AC or press ESC.
