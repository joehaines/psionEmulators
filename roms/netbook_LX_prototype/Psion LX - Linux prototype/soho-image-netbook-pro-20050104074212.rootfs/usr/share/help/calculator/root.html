Roots and Number to the Power of.



  • To calculate the square of a number in algebraic notation mode, enter the number, press the sqrt key and press Enter.
  • To calculate a number to a given power in formula notation mode, enter the number, press the x^y key, enter the power and press Enter.

Trigonometric Functions.

  • To calculate the sine, cosine, or tangent of a number, tap the sine, cosine or tangent keys. The function name together with an opening bracket will appear in the text entry field. Enter the number, a closing bracket and press Enter.

Note: The same principle of data entry applies where you want to calculate inverse sine, cosine and tangent, hyperbolic sine, cosine and tangent and inverse hyperbolic sine, cosine and tangent.
