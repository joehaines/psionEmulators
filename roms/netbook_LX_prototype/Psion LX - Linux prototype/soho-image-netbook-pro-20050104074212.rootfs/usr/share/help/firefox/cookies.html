Cookies



Cookies are small files which store your personalised information and settings for a Web site. Other sites may read the information in your cookie files, so although they are normally harmless, you may want to stop using them for confidentiality reasons or in order to monitor their activities.

If you want to monitor cookies on a case-by-case basis:

  • select the Privacy tab (go to Tools/Downloads/Preferences), click on the Cookies button, Enable Cookies and press the Ask for each cookie button before clicking OK.
  • To disable cookie activity toggle the Cookies button off.
