Using The Stylus.



The stylus is located in a recess in the right side of the computer. To remove it, push the square button next to the stylus and the stylus will pop out. To store it, push the stylus back into the recess.

Tap lightly on the screen using the stylus. You can use your fingers to tap on the screen, though it is best to use the stylus to avoid fingerprints or smudges on the screen. Do not tap the screen with any object that has a sharp tip or you may damage the screen. Don't use any type of ink pen, as the ink may be impossible to remove. You can usually tap on (click on) an item with the stylus to select or change it. For example, you can:

  1. Tap (or click) a program icon, to open a program.
  2. Press the Alt key, then tap, to right-click.

Note: If the screen doesn't respond to your taps, you may need to recalibrate it by selecting the Screen Setup option from the Control Panel or by pressing Fn-C.
