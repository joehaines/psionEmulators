Moving around in the working area.



A Gnumeric Workbook may contain more than one Worksheet which the user can move between by tapping the appropriate Sheet tab below the Cell Grid. These will initially be labelled as Sheet1, Sheet2 and Sheet3. Worksheets can also be:

  • Named or renamed by double tapping on a Worksheet tab.
  • Removed and duplicated by selecting Sheet from the Edit Menu or by tapping and holding the stylus over the tab to reveal the Manage Sheets menu.

The Cell Grid Area



Information is displayed in cells on a worksheet. Cells are referred to by their position in the grid of columns and rows, e.g. D3 refers to the cell in column D and row 3.

Moving Around the Cell Grid



To enter information in a cell, tap on that cell or type your information in the Data Entry Area. The selected cell is marked by a black border. You can use the scrollbars or the arrow keys to move to other areas of the Worksheet. Tab selects the next cell in the row. Enter selects the next cell in the column. Select Search from the Edit Menu to move to a cell whose content is known or select the Go to cell option from the Edit Menu for transfer to a specific cell.

Note: You can alter the number of cells displayed by using the zoom commands, or by setting the zoom level using the command on the View menu.

Highlighting a group of cells, a column or row is also possible:

  • To highlight more than one cell: drag the stylus from the first cell in the block to cover the desired cells, or hold down Shift and use the arrow keys.
  • To highlight cells which are not adjacent to each other on the cell grid, press Ctrl and make the selection with the stylus.
  • To highlight a whole column or a whole row: tap the shaded column or row heading, e.g. C or 2.
  • To highlight the entire worksheet: hold down Ctrl and press A.

Customising the Cell Grid



You can change the width of columns and the height of rows to suit the information contained in them by selecting the Column or Row option from the Format menu.

  • To freeze panes on the worksheet so that the selected part of the sheet remains static in relation to the other parts, highlight the information you wish to freeze in the rows and columns and select Freeze panes.

Note: If you enter more text than can be displayed in the current cell, the information will run over into the next column if that column is empty, so you may want to adjust the column width.
