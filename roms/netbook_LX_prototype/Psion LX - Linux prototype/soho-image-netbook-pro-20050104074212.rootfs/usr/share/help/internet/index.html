Connecting to internet.



Before Web and Email can be used on your Psion LX, you need to set up ISP and Email account details.

You can use your Psion LX to:

  • Access the Internet and view web pages.
  • View locally stored web pages and graphics files.
  • Download files e.g. additional programs for your Psion LX.
