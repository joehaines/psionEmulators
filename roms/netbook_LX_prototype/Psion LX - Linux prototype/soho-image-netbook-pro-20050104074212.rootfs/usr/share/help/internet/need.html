What do I need to use Web/Email?



To access the Internet and send and receive email on your Psion LX you must have the following:

  1. An appropriate Email account.
  2. A modem.
  3. A telephone or network connection.

You will need a modem to connect your Psion LX to the telephone network. You can use the following types of modem with your Psion LX:

  • Standard external PC modems.
  • PC Card modems.

If you are using a standard external modem or PC Card modem, you will need to connect your modem to a telephone socket.
