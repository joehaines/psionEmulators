Word

The menu and toolbar in Word should be extremely familiar to users of Microsoft Word™ (or other PC word processors, for that matter). Some differences:
  • To see how a document would look printed, choose "Print layout" on the "View" menu. There is no separate Print preview.
  • Word does not support absolutely all of Microsoft Word's Table features, just the most common.
  • The Psion LX uses the 'Albany' and 'Thorndale' fonts, which are replacements for 'Arial'™ and 'Times New Roman'™.
  • Some of the more esoteric menu commands and mouse actions have been left out.
