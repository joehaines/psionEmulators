# netbook: the v0.11 YModem bootloader image.  Real netBook hardware

# netbook_full: the v0.11 YModem bootloader + the actual netBook EPOC R5
# OS image attached as a CF card.  netBookLoadOsFromCard (in
# core/sa1100.cpp) does the bootloader -> OS handoff on attach: scans
# the CF for the "EPOCARM ROM" magic, strips the 256-byte header, copies
# the body to RAM bank 1 at PA 0xC8000000, disables the MMU, sets the
# boot-arg registers and jumps to 0xC8000000.  The OS then runs through
# its own cold boot.  In 93 sim-sec we see ETest.exe + WSERV reach
# their hot loops, OSMR1 ticks fire 17k+ times, and the LCD draws the
# Psion desktop with the clock ticking once a minute.
# Variance 5187, 14 unique pixel values, animation across ~3-4 frame
# hashes over the post-attach window.
netbook_full | netBook_BL_v011_eng.bin       |  3 | 5000 | --card-path roms/netbook_os.img --post-attach-seconds 90 | netbook
