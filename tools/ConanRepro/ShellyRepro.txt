IMPORTANT NOTES: Repro Shelly from PC running NT4

Scenario + notes
================

	* For use with prototype Conan machines *ONLY*
	* Need docking station
	* Need DC power supply (DC Jack)
	* Only works on ER5 based machines at present

PC-side executable instructions
===============================

     	* Use PsiWin to download the SRepro.exe
	* Disconnect PsiWin by right-clicking on the PsiWin icon (bottom right)
	* Open DOS box and Execute W32REPRO.EXE  

	  eg:  C:>W32REPRO -P1 -RAW CONEY.BIN  

ARM executable instructions
===========================

	* Do the PC-side first (as described above)
	* Make sure W32REPRO.EXE running and the Mains Power (DC Jack) connected
	* Execute SRepro.exe   
        * Wait for big PASS, may take 20-30 minutes.
	* Perform a hard reset on the unit

Testing the bootloader
======================
	
	* Wait until Conan machine prompts, "Please make a serial connection"
	* Open Hyperterminal on PC
	* Configure port to 115kB, 8 data bits, no parity, 1 stop bit
	* Wait until you see, "Psion YModem Bootloader 00" on Hyperterminal
	* Click "Transfer", "Send File", "Browse"
	* Select "test.img"
	* Select Protocol, "YModem"
	* Click "Send"
	* Wait for sending to complete, then observe text shell bootup
